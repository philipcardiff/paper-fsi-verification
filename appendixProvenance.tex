\paragraph{Reproducibility of the drivers.}
Table~\ref{tab:driver_properties} states which of the properties recommended in Section~\ref{sec:infrastructure} the present drivers implement.
Every driver exits with a non-zero status when a criterion fails and writes its QoIs to CSV, and every case has a machine-readable reference file that records its source.
The extraction method is recorded explicitly for the 3-D pressure-pulse tube, the Turek--Hron benchmarks and the beam in cross-flow, and the collapsible channel stores the oomph-lib driver that generates its reference.
No driver records the compiler or its flags; where they are known, they are given in Table~\ref{tab:provenance}.
