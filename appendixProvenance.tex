\section{Provenance of the results}
\label{app:provenance}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
% Moved from Section 4.3 (review of Sections 1-4, November 2026).

Table~\ref{tab:provenance} records, for each result, the solids4foam commit, the OpenFOAM release, the compiler and the platform, and whether the build contains the corrections of the aliasing and interpolation-weight defects (Appendix~\ref{app:lessons}).
Results computed without these corrections are affected only where an interface is three-dimensional and transferred non-conformally, or where the aliasing defect can occur (\texttt{-O3} builds); this is checked case by case in Section~\ref{sec:benchmark_suite}.
