### 1.4 Pass/fail rule for Stage B (fixed now)
Primary geometry G1 (root 0.248990), finest hex8 level (nt = 16), both CSM1 and CSM2:
 PB1 uy: |uy_FE - uy_ref| / |uy_ref| <= 1.0 %.
 PB2 ux: |ux_FE - ux_ref| / |ux_ref| <= 2.0 % (relative). Reason (stated before running): ux is the axial displacement dominated by bending foreshortening, a quantity second order in the slope (ux ~ -(1/2) integral w'^2 dx plus a small axial strain), so a relative error eps in uy produces about 2 eps in ux; a 1 % uy tolerance therefore corresponds to about 2 % in ux. I also report whether ux <= 1 % relative, and |dux| as a fraction of |uy_ref| (informational, the "scale" form). For CSM2, |ux_ref| = 0.469 mm is 2.8 % of |uy_ref|: a 2 % relative ux tolerance is 9.4 microns (0.055 % of |uy_ref|), which is the strictest of the alternatives.
 PB3 equilibrium and implementation checks (all runs): |sum Ry / W - 1| <= 1e-6 with W = rho |g| L h TZ (exact reference volume), |sum Rx| <= 1e-6 W; uy(A) < 0 and Ry > 0 (direction); max |uz| <= 1e-12 m; every load step converges with the declared tolerances; mean Newton iterations per step <= 8, no cut-back; no negative Jacobian.

 PB5 hex20 cross-check (reported; used for diagnosis, not as the pass criterion): hex20 nt = 4 uy within 0.5 % of the reference; |hex8 nt16 - hex20 nt4| / |ref| reported as the locking estimate.
Stage B PASSES iff PB1, PB2, PB3 hold for both CSM1 and CSM2 (PB4 and PB5 are reported). ### 3.3 Pre-registered post-processing definitions (all from the logged nodal data of the z = 0 layer: wx, wy, ef at every step; p = -K ef; 2-D quads of `mesh2d.npz`; boundary edges oriented with the interior on the left; n_f = outward normal of the fluid (into the obstacle) = (dy, -dx)/len)
Force on the obstacle (fluid on body) per unit depth: F = integral over the obstacle wall of (p n_f - 2 mu D n_f) dS, D = symmetric velocity gradient.
 F1 (wall-integrated, "solver-applied-equivalent": the FEBio fluid-FSI traction would assemble, from facet nodal dilatation, the pressure -K e_f at the facet nodes and the viscous traction from the element velocity gradient at the facet integration points; there is no FSI in CFD1, so F1 is a RECONSTRUCTION of that force, not a solver output, NOT TESTED against the real FSI routine): pressure = integral of the linear nodal interpolant over each facet (trapezoid) + viscous = 2 mu D n_f evaluated at the two Gauss points of the facet from the adjacent bilinear quad (one-sided), 2-point Gauss.
 F2 (reconstructed with element averages, the plot-pathway definition): pressure = mean of the four nodal pressures of the adjacent element times facet area; viscous = 2 mu D n_f with D at the centre of the adjacent element.
 Components reported: pressure and viscous separately for F1 and F2, on the cylinder and on the flag (top, bottom, tip) separately, drag = Fx, lift = Fy.
 F3 (control-volume momentum balance, independent of the obstacle-wall pressure): F = integral over inlet and outlet of rho u (u.n) dS - integral over inlet, outlet, top and bottom of (-p n + 2 mu D n) dS, n outward (steady state), same discretisation (nodal p trapezoid, D from the adjacent quad at Gauss points, u linear, 2-point Gauss).
Mass: Q_in = - integral u.n over the inlet, Q_out over the outlet (exact for linear u), and the flux through the vertical grid line x = 1.0 (nodes sorted by y); imbalance |Q_out/Q_in - 1| and |Q(1.0)/Q_in - 1|; Q_in must equal 0.082 g(t) to 1e-6.
Pressure parity on the flag walls (top y = 0.21 and bottom y = 0.19; wall nodes sorted by x, excluding nodes within three element lengths of the root and the tip corners; tip wall separate) — two separate measures, both in Pa:
 (P1) alternating along-wall amplitude a_t = rms over interior wall nodes of [p_k - (p_{k-1} + p_{k+1})/2]/2 (equals the amplitude of a pure odd-even mode; smooth curvature contributes O(h^2 p'')); repeated on the first and second node rows above/below the wall.
 (P2) wall-normal row offset D_n = mean over the same wall nodes of [p_wall - p_extrap], p_extrap = quadratic Lagrange extrapolation to the wall of the nodal pressures of the first three nodes along the quad-connectivity normal chain (distance coordinates from the mesh); its along-wall rms is reported as spread. D_n is the systematic offset that biased the nodal-pressure force in the cavity (load change = offset x length).
 Physical cross-flag pressure difference (kept separate from spurious modes): dp(x) = p_top(x) - p_bottom(x) at nodes of equal x on the two flag faces (the flag top/bottom grids are mirror images), its mean q (Pa), rms and the transverse force integral; the spurious fractions are a_t/rms(dp) and D_n/q.
 The same P1 measure along the cylinder wall (ordered contour) is reported for completeness. A small parity amplitude is NOT treated as proof of a correct integrated force: force tests F1/F2/F3 and the parity tests are evaluated independently.
Convergence: Newton iterations per step from the log ("Nonlinear solution status" blocks), termination from the log (never the exit code), steady-state spread over the last 20 % of the run (t in [32, 40] s) of drag and lift from F1 at every step.

### 4.3 Diagnosis from the existing outputs only (no further job)
D1. PROVEN (data): the pressure field carries a grid-scale odd-even mode over the whole downstream channel. Along every vertical grid line from x = 0.6 to 2.4 the nodal pressure alternates node by node in y with amplitude (rms second difference / 2) 20-23 Pa near x = 0.6, 16 Pa at x = 1.0, decaying to 1.4 Pa at x = 2.38 (column means alternate too: 25.8, 29.8, 26.2, ... in x), against smooth pressures of 25-30 Pa (jump of about +-16 Pa at x = 1.0: p = 39.0, 7.7, 39.1, 7.7, ... for successive nodes, while the velocity profile ux is smooth, 0.08, 0.15, 0.20, 0.24, ...). Because the number of node rows across the channel is even/odd, the bottom wall rows carry +16 Pa and the top wall rows -16 Pa (wall pressures at x = 0.6..1.0: bottom 40-50, top 6-9 Pa). It is the cavity's row-parity mode (FEBIO_PRESSURE_MODE_ANALYSIS.md) at a much larger relative amplitude (cavity: 0.36 Pa on 1.2 Pa, about 30 %; here about 65 % of the smooth pressure) and in both mesh directions.
D2. Consequences for the forces: the element-mean pressure (F2, F3b) averages the odd-even mode out exactly along a row; the nodal-pressure wall integral (F1) does not. Drag: F1 +15.7 %, F2 -2.1 %, F3b +2.6 %; lift: F1 2.88 (+157 %), F2 1.107 (-1.1 %), F3b 1.117 (-0.2 %) against the CFD1 reference 14.2929 / 1.11905: the parity-free reconstructions agree with the Featflow CFD1 values to 2-3 % in drag and 1 % in lift at level 1 (SUPPORTED, single level, NO convergence claim; the F2/F3b spread of 4.6 % in drag is itself a consistency failure of the reconstructions at level 1), the nodal-pressure force is off by 16 % and the nodal control volume F3 by 18 % (drag) and a factor of 40 (lift: the +-16 Pa wall offsets multiply the 2.5 m channel wall length).
