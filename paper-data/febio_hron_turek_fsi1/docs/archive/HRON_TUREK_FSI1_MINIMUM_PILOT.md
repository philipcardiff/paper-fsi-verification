## 1. Why this pilot, and what it can decide
FSI1 is the one remaining candidate with (a) unmodified like-for-like equivalence for a monolithic FEBio solid (SVK, force-consistent traction, audit rows 5-6), (b) an independent six-level reference (Featflow, converged to about 0.01 %), (c) an explicit manuscript gap (two levels only; "third level desirable"), (d) a cheap hierarchy of decoupled sub-tests with their own published references (CSM1/CSM2 for the structure, CFD1 for the rigid-flag fluid). Its principal danger is analytical: the flag-driven QoI uy(A) = 0.82 mm is set by a net load of about 0.48 Pa (audit row 7) on a near-flag pressure scale of 20-100 Pa, i.e. 0.5-2.5 %, while the cavity record shows pressure odd-even amplitudes of 2-6 % of the interface pressure and a nodal-load force bias of 5-13 %. Stage C is designed to measure exactly that on the rigid flag, before any coupled run is paid for. Expected outcome distribution (UNVERIFIED, my judgment from the cavity record): structure (B) passes with probability about 0.8; fluid drag (C) passes with probability about 0.6; the pressure-parity gate on the flag walls (C) passes with probability about 0.4; coupled uy then 0.3 conditional on the gates. A reproducible failure of the parity gate with a numerical diagnosis is itself a useful result (DIAGNOSTIC DISCREPANCY for lift and uy) and is a planned outcome of stage C.

## 3. Common set-up (all FEBio stages)
- Model: 2-D by one-element-thick hex8 slab (thickness TZ = 0.015 for continuity with the tutorial forces, u_z = 0 on all nodes, w_z = 0 for the fluid), per-unit-depth force = slab force/TZ (the nodal-integrals along x need no TZ factor, as in `tools/cavity_traction.py`).
- Fluid: `fluid` mixture inside the `fluid-FSI` domain (Stage D) or fluid-only domain (Stage C), rho = 1000, mu = 1.0 Pa s, `k` (viscosity bulk) 0, bulk modulus K = 1e6 Pa pre-declared (K sensitivity K = 1e7 at level 1 only; e_f = p/K about 1e-4 at p = 100 Pa; Mach number 3e-4; cavity floor lesson: relaxed tests, max_refs increased as in Womersley B1b if and only if a stall occurs, recorded as a deviation). `rhoi` = 0 (as cavity). Newton tolerances as in the validated ring/cavity decks (etol 1e-4 to 1e-6, rtol; exact values fixed in the generator metadata before the first run); relaxed tests (vtol = ftol = dtol = 0 with rtol only) are allowed as a declared fallback, not as a first choice.
- Inlet: `fluid normal velocity` with `parabolic`, rim pressure, magnitude from a load curve g(t) x v0 with v0 chosen so that the DISCRETE nodal-flux equals 0.2 x 0.41 g(t) = 0.082 g(t) m^2/s exactly (nodal parabola factor 1/(1 - sum d^3/H^3); assertion of inlet flux at every step; report flux at x = 1.0 and 2.5). Ramp g(t) = (1 - cos(pi t/2))/2 for t <= 2 s then 1 (load curve with points at all step times if `math` has no conditional). Outlet nodes: `zero fluid dilatation` (p = 0). Walls and cylinder: zero velocity. Corner/pin nodes where all velocity DOFs are fixed (inlet/outlet-wall corners, flag-root/cylinder junction): pin dilatation value taken from a clean neighbouring/unpinned reference, declared, with a pin-value sensitivity (x0.5, x2) at level 1, as in the cavity.
- Solid: `isotropic elastic`, E = 1.4e6, v = 0.4, density 1000; clamp at the root face x = 0.248990 (all nodes of that face fixed in x, y); flag nodes on the wetted faces shared with the fluid mesh (conformal).
- Mesh motion (stage D): massless regulariser solid with E_mesh = 1e-3 x flag modulus scale as in the ring D4 and cavity (declared, one value; sensitivity at level 1 only).
- Forces (no reaction force available): F1 = nodal e_f pressure (K e_f) integrated with the trapezoid rule over the wetted facets, plus viscous traction 2 mu D n from the element-mean rate of deformation; F2 = element-mean pressure; F3 = control-volume momentum balance on the whole channel: F_obstacle = (inlet momentum flux - outlet momentum flux) + (pressure and viscous traction integrals on the channel walls and the open boundaries) (independent of the wall-pressure parity because it uses inlet/outlet/channel-wall data); wall shear by a 3-point one-sided fit through the first nodes of the wall-normal line as in `tools/cavity_traction.py`. Report drag and lift per unit depth, split pressure/viscous, on cylinder and on flag separately.
- Time integration: generalised alpha, rhoi 0, dt = 0.1 s for t <= 2 (20 steps), then dt = 0.5 s (stage C) or 0.1 s (stage D) to the end. Steady-state criterion fixed beforehand: relative spread of uy(A), ux(A), drag over the last 20 % of the run below 1e-3 (the driver's own criterion in the tutorial) and fluid-solid interface node velocity below 1e-6 m/s.
- Slurm: one job at a time (or two small jobs under the 8-core aggregate), 2-4 cores, never 1, `tools/submit_guarded.sh`, ledger via `tools/ledger_add.sh`/`ledger_update.py`, pre-submission input-hash registration; no direct execution on the login node.

### Stage B: structural-only flag (cheap, FEBio solid module, 2 cores)
Purpose: independent check of SVK, plane-strain implementation, root clamp, hex8 locking and mesh sensitivity of the flag compliance before any fluid is involved; the CSM1/CSM2 steady values of Featflow are the reference; the FSI1 load regime (linear) is tested separately.
Model: the tutorial flag geometry (root x = 0.248990, length 0.351010, 0.02 thick), clamped root face, body force rho g with g = -2 m/s^2 in y (CSM1: E = 1.4e6; CSM2: E = 5.6e6; CSM1/CSM2 rho 1000, v 0.4). Meshes: n_t x n_x = 2x35, 4x70, 8x140, 16x280 (aspect 1:1; 70, 280, 1120, 4480 hex8 elements; 2-D slab). Steady (static) with load ramp in 10 steps. hex20 (same 4x70, 8x140) as a self-consistency, labelled as such.
Tests and acceptance:
 B1 linear-regime analytic: g scaled to 0.02 m/s^2 (linear): uy compared with the plane-strain Euler-Bernoulli value q L^4/(8 E' I') (q = rho g h; L = 0.351010) PLUS the known Timoshenko shear correction for h/L = 0.057 (about 1.3 x (h/L)^2 x E'/G(...) estimate computed in the generator), accepted as a check of both implementation and locking: finest level within 0.5 % of the analytic-with-shear value; 4x70 within 2 %; error decreasing monotonically with order 1.5-2.5 from the 2/4/8/16 sequence.
 B2 CSM1 (SVK, g = 2): finest hex8 uy within 1.0 % and ux within 2.0 % of -66.1029e-3 and -7.18777e-3 m (Featflow 4+3); CSM2: uy within 1.0 %, ux within 3.0 % of -16.9740e-3 and -0.469006e-3 m. Reported with the UA caveat on the Featflow root geometry (audit row 19). Observed order from levels 2/4/8/16 with Richardson extrapolation reported.
 B3 plane strain and constants: simple shear and confined compression check recover mu = 0.5e6 and lambda + 2 mu = 3.0e6 Pa to 1e-6; u_z = 0 everywhere to 1e-12.
 B4 load-convention check for the later transfer: a uniform pressure of 0.48 Pa on the lower face of the flag through `fluid-FSI traction`-equivalent surface load or a plain pressure load: tip deflection against B1 at 1e-3 (checks that the nodal consistent load on the hex8 faces is the same as the analytic load).
Stop conditions B: any of B3 fails (material mismatch), B1/B2 finest level outside the stated tolerance with the 16-element level (indicates a root/geometry or implementation issue: investigate before C), non-convergent Newton. If only the 2/4-element levels fail but 8/16 pass, locking is documented and the mesh levels for D are chosen at n_t >= 6 (decision rule fixed now: use the finest n_t with error <= 1 % in B1).
Cost: seconds to minutes each; total about 0.2-0.3 CPU-h (2 cores; cavity plate-only runs and `S1_hex8_plates` for scale).

## 6. Outcome classification used (per quantity)
- VERIFIED AGREEMENT: three mesh levels, observed order in [1, 2.5] or a monotone decreasing error, finest-level error and Richardson-extrapolated error both within the stated tolerance (uy 3 % - the maintainers' 4x tolerance; drag 1 %; ux 5 %; lift 5 %), time-step sensitivity <= 0.5 %, K sensitivity <= 0.5 %, like-for-like model (true here), force route consistency (F1 vs F2 vs F3) <= 1 % of the quantity.
- QUALIFIED AGREEMENT: within the tolerance but with fewer than three levels, or with a documented unresolved numerical difference (parity bias, one route only).
- DIAGNOSTIC DISCREPANCY: reproducible disagreement with a mechanism supported by numbers (pressure parity, F1/F2 split, structural locking), i.e. a limitation of the FEBio formulation rather than a statement about solids4foam.
- NOT ESTABLISHED: gate failure, non-convergence, or the numerical floor exceeds the quantity (e.g. parity amplitude above the 0.48 Pa signal).
Different classes per quantity are expected: structure (B) and drag are the likeliest to reach VERIFIED/QUALIFIED; ux(A) next; uy(A) and lift are the most exposed to the force floor. Agreement without mesh or time convergence is never called a completed verification.
