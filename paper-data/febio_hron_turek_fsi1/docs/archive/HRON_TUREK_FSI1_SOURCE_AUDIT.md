2. All requested FSI1 parameters are confirmed from files or source (table, section 2): St Venant-Kirchhoff total-Lagrangian solid, plane strain (`planeStress no`, 2-D `empty` patches, one spanwise cell 0.015 m), E = 1.4 MPa, nu = 0.4, rho_s = 1000, rho_f = 1000, nu_f = 1e-3, mean inflow 0.2 m/s (peak `maxValue` 0.3 of a parabola over 0..0.41 m), cosine ramp over 2 s, outlet p = 0 (fixed value) with zero-gradient velocity. 4. The "reference accuracy 0.14-0.26 %" in earlier notes is NOT an uncertainty. It is the spread between the coarsest level (2+0) and the finest (7+0): ux 0.73 %, uy 0.19 %, drag 0.14 %, lift 0.26 % (re-computed, section 3). Differences between the finest levels are far smaller: 6+0 to 7+0 changes ux 0.015 %, uy 0.0027 %, drag 0.0017 %, lift 0.0013 %. The remaining discretisation error of the level-7+0 values is of the order 0.02 % (ux), 0.003 % (uy), 0.004 % (drag, non-monotone), 0.0005 % (lift) (AE from the table differences). A conservative reference uncertainty for acceptance purposes: 0.05 % (ux), 0.01 % (uy, drag, lift). NE.
5. Strict like-for-like IS available in unmodified FEBio v4.13 for the solid: `isotropic elastic` is SVK total Lagrangian, the benchmark itself specifies SVK, plane strain follows from a one-element-thick slab with u_z = 0, and the force-consistent current-area traction convention is the same class (s4f `useUndeformedArea` defaults to false). Additionally the problem is practically linear (AE row 7), so the model-form gap that disqualified blobInTreacle does not exist. Remaining differences are numerical only (cell-centred FV solid with diffStencil stabilisation vs hex8 FE; segregated IQN-ILS vs monolithic; first-order one-sided wall traction vs FEBio nodal-pressure traction; FEBio's weakly compressible fluid).
6. MD (manuscript discrepancies, not edited): (a) the manuscript (`sectionBenchmarkSuite.tex` l.~224) and `paper-data/benchmark_inventory.md` call FSI1 "large (StVK)" / "small but geometrically nonlinear deformation"; the actual deflection is 0.82 mm of a 351 mm flag (AE: geometric nonlinearity about 1e-5). ## 4. What the audit implies for an FEBio test (carried into HRON_TUREK_FSI1_MINIMUM_PILOT.md)
- Equivalent in model: geometry, fluid law, SVK, plane strain, inlet flux function, p = 0 outlet, no-slip, point A, force definition, load convention.
- Different numerically (reported, never tuned): cell-centred FV vs hex8 FE with equal-order velocity/dilatation (parity mode, weak compressibility); s4f solid stabilisation vs none; segregated IQN-ILS vs monolithic; first-order wall traction vs nodal pressure; s4f fluid mesh non-conformal at the flag, FEBio conformal.
- Not available in FEBio: small-strain domain (not needed), xplt reaction forces in fluid-FSI (use F1/F2 plus a control-volume balance), `linear constraint` for fluid DOFs (not needed).
- Principal scientific risk (AE): the flag-driven quantities (uy = 0.82 mm) are set by a net differential load of about 0.48 Pa on an absolute near-flag pressure scale of order 20-100 Pa (drag 14.3 N/m over a 0.1 m cylinder gives about 140 Pa), a 0.5-2.5 % differential. The cavity showed odd-even pressure amplitudes of 2-6 % of the interface pressure and a force bias of 5-13 % through the nodal-pressure load, although displacements converged at order 1.9 there. FSI1 is therefore a harder test of the parity/force-floor problem than the cavity; ux(A) (axial) and the total drag (a 14 N/m integral dominated by the cylinder) are expected to be more robust than uy and lift. This risk is evaluated by stage C before any coupled run.
