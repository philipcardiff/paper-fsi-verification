# Scientific summary

Scope: independent FEBio v4.13 (stock, unmodified) evidence on the Hron-Turek FSI1 benchmark (Re = 20, steady, E_s = 1.4 MPa, nu_s = 0.4, rho_s = rho_f = 1000, nu_f = 1e-3, mean inflow 0.2 m/s). No coupled run. Two decoupled stages were run against the Featflow tables (retrieved 2026-10-31): stage B, the flag alone (CSM1/CSM2); stage C, rigid cylinder and flag in the FSI1 channel (CFD1-type). The tutorial reference is solids4foam `development` b200718f4f8390c90f9c7e3ded8b74f3e13941cc (source audit in `docs/archive/HRON_TUREK_FSI1_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`).

## 1. What was established
1. **Model equivalence of the structure** (CONFIRMED FROM SOURCE and INPUTS): the tutorial uses St Venant-Kirchhoff total-Lagrangian plane strain (E 1.4e6, nu 0.4, rho 1000, `planeStress no`) and FEBio's `St.Venant-Kirchhoff` is the same law; the interface load convention (force-consistent, current area) is the same class. FSI1 is practically linear (tip slope 3.1e-3 rad; analytical estimate).
2. **Stage B** (SUPPORTED): CSM1 and CSM2 tip displacements agree with the Featflow references to 0.2-0.5 % (hex8, 4,496 elements) and 0.08-0.16 % (hex20, 280 elements), converging monotonically with observed order 1.84 (`results/stageB_*.csv`, `figures/fig1`). The geometry with the flag root on the cylinder circle (x = 0.24899) was fixed before the runs from the tutorial and the Featflow definition (flag "fully attached to the fixed cylinder"); the nominal-length variant (root 0.25) was run as a labelled diagnostic and is 1.3 % too stiff.
3. **Stage C** (PROVEN as data): with K = 1e6, dt = 0.5 s, 80 steps, level 1, the pressure field has a grid-scale odd-even mode (amplitude 15.8 Pa along x = 1.0 on a mean 23.5 Pa; 2.6-4.1 Pa along the flag walls; node-by-node alternation 39.0, 7.6, 39.0, ... Pa across the channel while the velocity profile is smooth). The mass balance is good (1.2e-4) and the solution is steady (1e-11).
4. **The nodal-pressure force fails** (PROVEN as data; mechanism SUPPORTED): the force obtained from the nodal dilatation on the wetted facets (the pressure part of FEBio's fluid-FSI traction formula, verified from source) is 16.54 N/m drag and 2.88 N/m lift, +15.7 % and +157 % against the CFD1 reference. Element-mean and weak-form reconstructions average the mode out and give 13.99-14.66 N/m drag and 1.107-1.117 N/m lift, but they are reconstructions that differ from each other by up to 4.8 % (budget in `docs/STAGE_C_CFD1.md`).
5. **Source audit** (CONFIRMED FROM SOURCE): the applied traction is `f = gt p - sv gt` with p from facet-interpolated NODAL dilatation and sv the element-mean viscous stress; it can be reconstructed from the stored CFD1 fields to numerical precision (`docs/FSI_TRACTION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`): 16.48 N/m drag, 2.87 N/m lift on the rigid-flag fields.

## 2. What was not established
- Any FSI1 coupled quantity (no coupled run; stage D was not authorised and is not recommended for the stock formulation).
- Mesh behaviour of the pressure mode (level 2 not run; the pre-registered stop rule ended stage C at level 1), its dependence on time step, ramp, K and run length (UNRESOLVED hypothesis: the mode is not damped and accumulates from the discrete divergence of the pseudo-transient), and the effect of the pin values (derived from polluted neighbours).
- Equilibrium of the structural solutions at the planned 1e-6 level (reaction forces unavailable for fixed DOFs; approximate free-body check only).
- The 0.1-0.3 % offset between the converged FEBio structural value and the Featflow value (hex8 Richardson +0.30 %, hex20 nt 4 +0.08 %).

## 3. Flag-driven signal versus the parity mode (analytical context)
The FSI1 tip deflection of 0.82 mm corresponds, for a uniformly loaded plane-strain cantilever (E' = 1.667e6 Pa, I' = 6.67e-7 m^3, L = 0.351 m), to a uniform transverse load of 0.48 Pa (total 0.17 N/m): ANALYTICAL ESTIMATE. The pressure difference between the two flag faces of the RIGID flag after cancelling the row-parity mode (two-row average) is -2.15 Pa on average (`results/parity_diagnostics_C1p_L1_f1.25_K1e6.json`; Euler-Bernoulli tip deflection from the element-mean pressure load of the rigid flag: 1.56 mm; from the nodal-pressure load: 11.6 mm). The raw nodal difference between the two faces is -10.6 Pa. These deflection numbers are ANALYTICAL DIAGNOSTICS (linear beam, pressure-only, rigid-flag loads, clamped root): they are not simulation results and 11.6 mm is not established.
