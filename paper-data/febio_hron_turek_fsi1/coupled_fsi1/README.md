# Coupled Hron-Turek FSI1 with FEBio v4.13 (added 2026-11-01): stock against a candidate load-transfer correction

**Status: qualified independent comparison obtained with a CANDIDATE modified build; stock FEBio v4.13 does not reproduce the benchmark. The correction is not verified for general use and is not proposed as a pull request.** All inputs here are the original FSI1 problem (Featflow reference u_x 2.2705e-5 m, u_y 8.2088e-4 m, category B in the manuscript; reference equality with the 2006 paper tables unconfirmed): same geometry, flag (St Venant-Kirchhoff, E 1.4e6 Pa, nu 0.4, rho 1000), fluid (rho 1000, mu 1 Pa s, weakly compressible K 1e6), no-slip walls and interface, the stage-C inlet/outlet formulation, pseudo-transient to the steady state (dt 0.5 s, run to 12 s; the plateau is reached at about 10 s).

## Result: steady tip displacement error against the Featflow reference (`results/coupled_table.md`, `figures/fsi1_coupled_tip_errors.png`)
| fluid factor | fluid cells | flag elements through thickness | stock u_y error | patched u_y error | stock u_x error | patched u_x error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.25 | 8,330 | 2 | +468.5 % | **-10.37 %** | -156.6 % | **+2.97 %** |
| 1.875 | 18,606 | 4 | +12.9 % | **-1.91 %** | +2.4 % | **+1.51 %** |
| 2.0 | 21,344 | 4 | -43.6 % | **-2.61 %** | -7.8 % | **+1.74 %** |
| 3.0 | 48,024 | 6 | -554.5 % | **-0.74 %** | -99.7 % | **+0.77 %** |
| 3.75 | 75,060 | 8 | -3.2 % | **-0.15 %** | +4.3 % | **+0.64 %** |

- **Stock FEBio is erratic in the fluid mesh**: u_y error +468 %, +13 %, -44 %, -554 % (u_y of the wrong sign) and -3.2 % at five levels (the value at factor 3.75 is not evidence of convergence: the factor 2.0 mesh, with the same flag resolution as 1.875, gives -44 %). The preregistered prediction that the stock error exceeds +100 % at every level **failed** (it is -3.2 % at factor 3.75).
- **With the correction the tip displacement converges monotonically with the flag resolution**: |u_y error| 10.4, 1.9, 0.74, 0.15 % at nt = 2, 4, 6, 8 (the 2.0 mesh, nt = 4: -2.6 %); u_x within 3.0 % at all levels, 0.64 % at the finest. For comparison, the manuscript's solids4foam FSI1 2x mesh has 0.97 % (u_x) and 4.64 % (u_y). Robustness at factor 1.875: pins at the stage-C rule values change the u_y error by 1e-4 percentage points, K = 1e7 by 2e-3 percentage points.
- **Why (derivation and tests in `docs/RT_PATCH_REPORT.md`)**: the stock traction applies the boundary integral of the nodal pressure while the discarded momentum residual at the no-slip interface nodes is the difference to the variationally consistent force; on the CFD1 geometry that difference is +12 to +24 % of the drag (and an unbounded error in the small lift) and varies erratically with the mesh (`../cfd1_refinement`). The candidate correction assembles that residual into the solid equations (viscous part counted once). In the ring benchmark it removes the net energy creation of the unresolved no-slip case (Gamma 1.4e-2 -> 1.8e-5) and leaves resolved results unchanged.

## What this does not establish
Verification of the correction (it failed one registered gate, P5, because of my test design, and a second, P5', through a viscous double count that a later variant removed; both kept in the record); a force-level measurement (root reaction logs were zero, so the predictions that compare the applied force were not evaluable); time-step independence (the steady state is independent of dt only to the extent that the plateau is reached; dt was not varied); the flag stiffness error (hex8 flag conformal with the fluid tip edge: -21 %, -6.3 % at nt 2 and 4 for the isolated flag, so the displacement errors at the coarse levels contain structural error of the same order and agreement at nt = 2 is partly a cancellation); incompressible limit (K 1e6 and 1e7); a single pin treatment (pins 0, and one rule check); generality to other geometries, free-slip or mixed interfaces, or other FEBio modules. The runs at factor 1.25 ended at 16 s, and the others were stopped at 12 s, because at the steady state the relative convergence tolerance has no attainable floor (declared fallback: displacement, velocity and dilatation criteria off, residual rtol 1e-10 and energy etol 1e-8 kept).

## Reproduce (no simulation)
`python3 scripts/coupled_tip.py` on a directory holding `pointA.txt` regenerates the tip values; `results/<run>/pointA.txt` and `inputs/*.feb.gz` are stored. The generator is `scripts/make_fsi1_coupled_feb.py` (needs the stage-C `make_hronturek_cfd_feb.py` and its block-mesh data). The patched build is NOT in this repository: `docs/rt_reaction_patch.diff` is the exact diff against the pristine v4.13 tree (three files, every change behind an environment switch that defaults to off), `docs/RT_BUILD_HASHES.txt` the library hashes. Preregistrations (hashed before the runs) are in `docs/`.
