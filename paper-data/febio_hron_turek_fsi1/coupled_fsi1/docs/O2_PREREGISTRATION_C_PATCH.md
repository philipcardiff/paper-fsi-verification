## P0 Mathematical derivation and source audit (established)
FEBio's fluid-FSI mixture domain writes the fluid momentum in strong pressure-gradient form: the w-row is R^w_a = (inertia) + integral N_a grad p dV + integral (viscous stress : grad N_a) dV (`FEFluidFSIDomain3D.cpp` ElementInternalForce, ElementInertialForce). At nodes where w is fixed (no-slip), that row has no equation: its value, the constraint reaction, is discarded, and the fluid acts on the solid only through the surface load `fluid-FSI traction`, which integrates the boundary form -p n dS + viscous term using the NODAL dilatation (`FEFluidFSITraction.cpp` LoadVector). Per element, divergence theorem: boundary integral of N_a p_h n dS = integral (N_a grad p_h + p_h grad N_a) dV. The two forms of the force therefore differ by the volume term integral p_h grad N_a over the elements adjacent to the interface, which vanishes for exact p and for nodal modes in the null space of the strong gradient (alternating modes cancel in integral N_a grad p_h but not in integral p_h grad N_a). Evidence from the CFD1 refinement (`B_GATE_RESULTS.md`): the variationally consistent nodal reaction F4 (the fluid's own w-row at the obstacle nodes, computed independently) is within 0.12-0.31 % (drag) and 0.3-0.6 % (lift) of the Featflow reference on all six meshes tested, while the boundary-integral force F1 is +12 to +24 % (drag) and erratic (lift) on five of six; and the Q1/Q1 operator audit (`q1q1_parity_operator.json`) shows exact alternating null modes of the strong-form coupling.
Correction under test: **when env FEBIO_RT_REACTION=1, the w-row residual and tangent rows of a node with w fixed and u free are assembled into that node's displacement equations** (variationally consistent reaction transfer, standard in monolithic FSI with shared nodes), and the `fluid-FSI traction` load is omitted from the input (otherwise the force is counted twice). With the switch off the code path is the stock one. Active only for no-slip (w-fixed) interfaces; free-slip interfaces, rigid walls (u fixed) and all other nodes are unchanged.

## P1 Minimal reproducer on stock (existing data)

A direct stock measurement of the applied load is not available (no reaction output at free-u nodes).

## Gates for the patched build (predictions fixed now)
- **P2 corrected reproducer**: on the 4-delta moderate-regime ring (G1 configuration, potential-flow start, no traction, switch on) the energy creation Gamma is reduced to at most 50 % of the stock value 1.41e-2 and the fitted decay is no longer negative.
