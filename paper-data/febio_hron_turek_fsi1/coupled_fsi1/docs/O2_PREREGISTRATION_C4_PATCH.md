# Overnight-2 preregistration C4 (exploratory, after C3): remove the double-counted viscous force

Hashed before the runs. C3 outcomes (fixed in `RT_PATCH_REPORT.md`): (a) uniform 12-cell no-slip, fluid at rest: stock ratio error against V -6.73 %, patched (reaction + traction) -2.05 %, decays 0.042 and 0.017 alpha_V; met (at most half, decay positive). (b) 4-delta, fluid at rest: stock -0.188 %, decay -0.517; patched -0.035 %, decay +0.439; met. (c) **failed**: the patched decay error does not fall with near-wall refinement: |decay/alpha_V - 1| = 0.067 (delta), 0.163 (delta/4), 0.170 (delta/16) against the stock's 0.02-0.03 at the resolved levels; the patched damping converges to about +17 % above the exact viscous rate.
Hypothesis for the persistent +17 %: the w-row assembled into the u equations contains the viscous force in weak form (sv:grad N), and the retained `fluid-FSI traction` contains the viscous boundary traction (element-mean sv n), so the viscous force on the ring is counted twice. By the derivation (F_consistent = F_traction + fe_w) the retained traction should supply the pressure boundary force and the reaction the strong-form pressure/inertia residual; the viscous force should enter once.
Test: new build (same patch plus env FEBIO_RT_NOVISC_TRACTION=1, which removes the viscous term from the traction residual and tangent; default off). Runs, patched with both switches on, traction retained: Pm1rt3 (production, fluid at rest, delta/4), G1rt3 (4 delta, fluid at rest), U1rt3 (uniform 12 cells, fluid at rest), Pd16rt3 (delta/16, fluid at rest).

Failed predictions are reported; a pass of P5'' and P2'' makes the correction a candidate, not a verified patch, because the regression on a case with a large strong-form residual (CFD1-type, free-u obstacle) has not been done.
