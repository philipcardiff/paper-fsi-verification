(the `febio4` launcher hash does not identify library changes; library hashes are in `RT_BUILD_HASHES.txt`).

## Mathematical basis (P0, established by derivation and source reading)
FEBio's fluid-FSI momentum row (w) is `ff = sv:grad N + (grad p) N` with fe = -ff (FEFluidFSIDomain3D.cpp ElementInternalForce, ElementInertialForce); at nodes with w fixed (no-slip) that row has no equation and is discarded. The fluid reaches the solid only through the surface load `fluid-FSI traction` (boundary integral of -p n plus the element-mean viscous traction, FEFluidFSITraction.cpp). With p_h the nodal-dilatation interpolant, the boundary integral of N_a p_h n dS equals the integral of (N_a grad p_h + p_h grad N_a), whereas the variationally consistent force is the weak form (pressure part: integral p_h grad N_a, plus inertia and viscous parts). The two differ by the strong-form residual at the constrained nodes, R_strong = integral N_a (rho a + grad p) dV, which is not enforced there. The candidate correction assembles the w-row (residual and tangent) of nodes with w fixed and u free into the displacement equations of the same node (`FEBIO_RT_REACTION=1`), which adds fe_w = -R_strong to the traction; because the w-row also contains the viscous force in weak form, the viscous part of the traction is removed (`FEBIO_RT_NOVISC_TRACTION=1`) so that it is counted once. Free-slip interfaces (w free), rigid walls (u fixed) and all other nodes are unaffected.

## What this shows and what it does not
- **Established (controlled intervention):** the net energy creation and the negative fitted damping of the unresolved no-slip ring are caused by the action-reaction inconsistency of the traction transfer: assembling the dropped w-row reaction (with the viscous force counted once) removes the energy creation entirely (Gamma 1.4e-2 -> 1.8e-5) and leaves the resolved results unchanged or slightly better (decay 1.002 and 1.038 alpha_V, ratio error against V within 0.006 %). This updates the earlier statement that the discrete mechanism was unidentified.
- **Candidate, not verified:** the correction as a fix. Not tested: a case with a large strong-form residual with free-u nodes beyond the coupled load-transfer test below; the incompressible limit; tolerance sensitivity; slip/no-slip mixed interfaces (the viscous-free traction switch is global and is valid only when all interface nodes are no-slip); node sets where u is fixed on part of the interface; shells; other FEBio modules. The unresolved uniform mesh is improved (-6.7 % -> -2.05 %) but remains outside the 0.1 % tolerance.
- The correction is NOT proposed as a pull request. Rejected prototypes (traction omitted; viscous double-counted) are preserved with their results.
