# Overnight-2 preregistration C5 (exploratory): load transferred to the flag in a coupled FSI1 model, stock against the reaction correction

Hashed before the runs. Context: the coupled FSI1 input (`tools/make_fsi1_coupled_feb.py`, stage-C fluid mesh at factor 1.25, flag conformal with the fluid mesh, 52 x 2 hex8 flag cells) ran with the stock build to a steady tip displacement u_y(A) = 4.667 mm (reference 0.8209 mm; the CSM test gives -21 % stiffness error for 2 hex8 elements through the thickness, so this mesh cannot verify the tip displacement) and then stopped at t = 16 s on the zero-initial-norm convergence floor of the displacement/velocity/dilatation criteria; the patched run stopped in its first step on the same floor (its residual converged to 2e-17). The flag mesh is tied to the fluid tip edge (2 cells at this level), so a structurally adequate coupled FSI1 needs a fluid factor of about 5 or a non-conformal coupling: this is the obstacle for the coupled benchmark. The test here is therefore of the LOAD TRANSFER only: for small deflection the reaction force at the clamped root equals the load applied to the flag, whatever the flag stiffness.
Reference reconstructions from the fluid-only run on the same mesh and pins (C3_f1.25, pins 0, `cfd1_flag_forces.py`): flag only, per metre depth, F1 = (2.597, 3.306), F2 = (2.433, 0.636), consistent reaction F4 (with junction nodes) = (2.594, 0.637) N/m.
Runs: stock coupled (FS1r_s) and patched (FS1r_p: FEBIO_RT_REACTION=1, FEBIO_RT_NOVISC_TRACTION=1), identical inputs and the declared relaxed convergence fallback (displacement, velocity and dilatation relative criteria off; residual rtol 1e-10 and energy etol 1e-8 kept), 80 steps, rhoi 0.
Predictions (fixed now): (R1) the stock root reaction per metre depth (sum of Rx, Ry over the root nodes divided by the slab thickness 0.015 m, sign reversed) is within 5 % of F1_flag = (2.597, 3.306) in both components; (R2) the patched root reaction is within 5 % (x) and 10 % (y) of F4_flag = (2.594, 0.637); (R3) the ratio of the patched to the stock steady tip u_y is within 30 % of 0.637/3.306 = 0.193 (the flag responds linearly at this small deflection); (R4) both runs complete 80/80 steps. A failed prediction is reported as failed. If R1 and R2 hold, the load-transfer claim is supported on a coupled model: the stock traction applies F1, the corrected transfer applies F4. This does not verify FSI1 (the flag structural error and one fluid level remain).
