# Overnight-2 preregistration C2 (addendum after a failed gate): reaction correction with the traction retained

Hashed before the runs. **Registered gate P5 of `O2_PREREGISTRATION_C_PATCH.md` FAILED**: the patched build with the switch on and the `fluid-FSI traction` load omitted gave, on the production ring (Pm1 configuration), a wet/dry ratio error against V of +7.08 % (stock -0.0049 %) and a decay 52.7 times the exact rate; P7 (switch off reproduces stock: omega to 2.3e-10, same iterations) and P3 (Newton 4.005 per step, residual 5e-3 -> 5e-21 in three iterations) were met; the two potential-flow-start runs (F2rt, G1rt) failed in their first step on the convergence-criterion floor and are reported as failed. That design was my error: the registration said the traction must be omitted to avoid double counting.
Post-failure analysis (stock output, `reaction_check.py`; derivation below): FEBio's w-row is ff = sv:grad N + (grad p) H (`FEFluidFSIDomain3D.cpp` ElementInternalForce), with fe = -ff. By the divergence theorem the boundary-integral traction and the strong-form w-row differ exactly by the strong-form residual: the variationally consistent force on the solid is F_consistent = F_traction - R_strong = F_traction + (assembled fe_w). On the ring the strong residual of the layer is small (the momentum equation holds in the layer; the work of the strong reaction at the interface nodes is 1.2e-8 against a ring energy swing of 1.0e-5, i.e. 0.12 %), so removing the traction removed the physical force, which is why the ring frequency moved towards the dry value; on the CFD1 rigid obstacle R_strong is the +12 to +24 % that separates F1 from F4. The existing patch already has the right sign for F_traction + fe_w (mapping the w-row onto the u equations), so the corrected use is: **switch on AND the traction load kept (stock input).** This is a new exploratory study registered after the failure; it does not rescue the failed registered gate.
Runs (patched build, FEBIO_RT_REACTION=1, stock inputs with the traction present, logging only added): Pm1rt2 (Pm1 input, fluid at rest), F2rt2 (resolved, potential-flow start, delta/4), G1rt2 (4 delta, potential-flow start).
Predictions (fixed now): (P3) 400/400 steps, mean Newton iterations at most 6, no reformation failure; (P5') Pm1rt2 ratio error against V within 0.05 pp of the stock value (-0.0049 %) and decay within 5 % of stock (0.984 alpha_V) - the strong residual is 0.12 % of the energy exchange, so the effect on the ring should be small; (P4) F2rt2 Gamma at most 3e-3; (P2') G1rt2 Gamma at most 50 % of stock 1.41e-2 and a decay that is not negative (exploratory: the mismatch need not be the sole cause). If P3 and P5' hold, the correction passes the ring regression and the remaining test of its purpose is a case where R_strong is large (CFD1-type); that case needs a coupled input and is not attempted unless P3, P4, P5' hold.
