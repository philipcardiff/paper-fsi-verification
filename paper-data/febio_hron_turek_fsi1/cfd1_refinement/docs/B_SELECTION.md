|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hron-Turek FSI1 (#28) | strongest: Featflow level 6+0 converged CFD1 and CSM numbers, published benchmark | structure CSM1/CSM2 to 0.2 to 0.5 % (hex8), 0.08 to 0.16 % (hex20); rigid CFD1 one level only | nodal-pressure wall force F1 (+15.7 % drag, +157 % lift) and a wall-row odd-even pressure at 8,330 cells; refinement never run | moderate: CFD1 refinement is cheap (about 1 to 12 CPU-h); a coupled FSI1 is a large, longer calculation | high: FSI1 is the classic benchmark | **SELECTED** (first gate: CFD1 refinement) |

| radialTorsionalRing (#29) | author's own reference, no independent nonlinear verification | static twist, fixed fluid; coupled stalled | coupled stall, reference uncertain | high, uncertain | medium-low | not selected |
Rationale: FSI1 has the best independent reference and already-verified structure; its stated open question (does the nodal traction converge under refinement?) is answerable with a small, decisive, preregistered test, and the ring result (nodal n = 2 force converging at first order, near-wall resolution decisive) gives a specific prediction. The lesson from the ring (unresolved layers and strongly constrained boundaries produce spurious behaviour that disappears under resolution) is the leading explanation to test, not an assumption.
