# Overnight-2 preregistration B3 (exploratory): is the nodal-pressure odd-even amplitude controlled by the parity of the block cell counts?

Hashed before the runs. B-stage outcome so far (fixed in `B_GATE_RESULTS.md`): preregistered B1 (monotone decrease of |F1 drag error|), B2 (L2 error at most 0.7 of L1) and B3 (odd-even amplitude falls by 1.5 from L1 to L2) all **failed**: F1 drag error +15.7 % (L1), -0.8 % (L1.5), +22.9 % (L2); cylinder odd-even amplitude 14.9, 6.5, 69.1 Pa; the element-mean reconstruction F2 converges monotonically (-2.1, -1.6, -1.2 % in drag). Pins do not explain it (L2 pinned and unpinned agree; at L1.5 pin variants change the F1 drag by up to 0.85 pp).
Observation (not a result): the generator scales each block's cell counts as round(n x factor); the number of blocks with an odd count is 19 (factor 1.25), 18 (1.875) and 6 (2.5), and the amplitude is largest where most counts are even. Hypothesis H-par: a pure alternation in the nodal pressure can close consistently around the obstacle and across blocks only if the counts are even, so the amplitude is larger on meshes with mostly even counts than on meshes of similar resolution with mostly odd counts.
Runs (pins 0 Pa at the two junction nodes, K 1e6, everything else as the B-stage; pin sensitivity at L1.5 was <= 0.9 pp in drag): f = 1.5 (11,996 cells, 6 odd), f = 1.625 (14,191, 29 odd), f = 2.0 (21,344, 0 odd), f = 2.375 (29,951, 29 odd), f = 3.0 (48,024, 26 odd).
Predictions: (P1) a_t(cylinder) at f = 1.5 is at least twice that at f = 1.625; (P2) a_t(cylinder) at f = 2.0 is at least twice that at f = 1.875 (6.5 Pa, odd 18) and at f = 2.375; (P3) a_t(cylinder) at f = 2.375 is at most half that at f = 2.5 (69 Pa); (P4) on the odd-rich meshes (1.625, 2.375, 3.0) |F1 drag error| is at most 5 % and decreases from 1.625 to 3.0. Soundness as B4. A prediction that fails is reported as failed. If P1-P3 hold, the finding is that mesh parity controls the mode (a mesh-design observation, not a remedy for the formulation); if they fail, parity is excluded as the controlling variable.
Role: exploratory; budget 8 CPU-h, 4 cores per run.
