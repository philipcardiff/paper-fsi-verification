## 2. The reference (category B: oomph-lib; CR, files above)
- oomph-lib monolithic Newton solver: Crouzeix-Raviart (Q2P-1) Navier-Stokes on an algebraically updated mesh, geometrically nonlinear Hermite Kirchhoff-Love beam (clamped, wall inertia Lambda^2 = rho_s U^2/E_eff = 2e-9, i.e. rho_s = 1), BDF2 (fluid) + Newmark (wall); the tutorial `refine 1`: 100 x 16 fluid elements, 40 beam elements. Parameters Re = ReSt = 50, Q = 4e-11, H = 0.05, sigma0 = 0, P_ext = 4e-7, T_ramp = 0.25, P_up = 300 (non-dim).
- Converged reference = Richardson extrapolation in time of dt = 0.003125 and 0.0015625; precision about 0.3 % of the peak 0.216 m (refinement 1 vs 2: 0.26 %; Richardson 0.06 %; dt 0.11 %; static 0.13 %). Stated precision of the oomph-lib beam against the continuum: 0.05 % (h/L = 0.005).
- Quantities extracted by this audit from the stored CSVs (wall midpoint, parabolic refinement through 5 points around the extremum; my extraction, exact values in `reference_data/cc/`): 
  - first trough: -0.2160 m at t = 0.4685 s (converged); at dt = 0.025: -0.2129 m at 0.4735 s (CSV raw -0.2130 at 0.475; the README quotes -0.21296); dt 0.0125: -0.2152 at 0.4703; dt 0.00625: -0.2157 at 0.4697; dt 0.003125: -0.2160 at 0.4691.
  - first rebound maximum: -0.0404 m at 0.791 s; second trough -0.2037 m at 1.119 s (dt 0.025: -0.2002 at 1.125, i.e. 1.7 % less); third trough -0.1936 at 1.753 s. Trough-to-trough period T = 0.650 s (1.119 - 0.4685), 0.634 s (third), so the README's "about 0.8 s" is the half-damped rebound period (the maximum at 0.791 s), not the trough spacing: FEBio periods are measured with the same extraction.
  - static clamped deflection under the full 200 Pa (wall alone): -0.143628 m (midpoint), -0.09645925 m at L/4 and 3L/4 (references.json). Difference from the Heil/oomph beam to the cubic continuum 0.05 %.
  - oomph's own temporal error at dt = 0.025: 6.3 % of the history peak (1.8 % at 0.0125, 0.49 % at 0.00625; observed order 1.8-1.9).
- solids4foam's own error (CR README/verification): tutorial mesh and step (cubic solid, IQN-ILS or Robin): first trough -0.21283/-0.2129 m at 0.47 s, 1.4 % from the converged -0.2160, 0.05-0.1 % from oomph at the same step (-0.21296), but the HISTORY error against the converged reference is 4.2-4.82 % (largest difference over t <= 1.5 s relative to 0.216 m); 4.65 % on the tutorial fluid mesh with Robin, falling to 0.50 % on the 4x-refined fluid meshes (1.43 % at 12,800 cells). The "4.2-4.65 %" error quoted in the reassessment is the HISTORY metric, not the trough error (the trough error is 1.4 %).

## 3. Differences that cannot be removed with stock FEBio v4.13
1. Fluid: weak compressibility (K), equal-order hex8 nodal-dilatation operator with the h^2-soft parity family (cavity F1 5-13 % below F2, FSI1 parity 2.6-4.1 Pa): the one mechanism that can bias the nodal fluid load. The collapsible channel is a SMOOTH, nearly-parallel channel flow, so the parity is expected small (UA until CC-F1).
2. Solid discretisation: hex8 finite-strain SVK with no cubic reconstruction: bending locking of a thin wall (S1).
3. Coupling algorithm and time integrator (monolithic gen-alpha vs partitioned IQN-ILS / BDF2 + Newmark): converge to the same continuous problem; only a time-step study can separate the temporal error (not authorised here).
4. Pressure-driven ends: imposed through the dilatation DOF (no `fluid` BC corresponding to the traction (P, 0)); the natural viscous term differs by O(mu du/dx).
5. The reference is a Kirchhoff-Love beam; both continuum codes carry the 0.05 % continuum-beam offset (accepted in the gate).

## 4. Verdict on like-for-like
Like-for-like on the physical problem (geometry, wall law, load, fluid, BCs, IC, time schedule, QoI definition) IS achievable in stock FEBio v4.13 for everything except (a) the hex8 wall discretisation at the tutorial's aspect-20 wall (decided by CC-S1; fallback 160 x 4 hex8 conformal with 160 fluid wall columns, a documented deviation in the fluid wall-region resolution: fluid 40/160/80 columns = 4,480 cells, 5,120 cells in total, the reassessment's "L1"), and (b) the equal-order pressure operator (decided by CC-F1). The comparison can be at most QUALIFIED AGREEMENT at one mesh level and one time step (verification needs a second mesh level and a dt check, not authorised).
