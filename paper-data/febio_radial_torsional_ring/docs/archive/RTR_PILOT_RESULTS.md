## 11. Source-level analysis requested by the coordinator (no runs)
**11.1 The t - dt/2 convention (where it applies).** `FEFluidSolver::InitStep` (fluid module): `ta = (t > 0) ? t - (1 - alpha_f) dt : alpha_f dt`, then `FESolver::InitStep(ta)` evaluates ALL load controllers (load curves, `lc` scaling of prescribed fluid velocities, `fluid normal velocity` surface loads, body-force curves) at ta; alpha_f = 1/(1 + rho_inf). rho_inf = 1 gives ta = t - dt/2 (our RT-B); rho_inf = 0 gives alpha_f = 1, ta = t: no offset. `FEFluidFSISolver::InitStep` (fluid-FSI module) calls `FESolver::InitStep(t)` (the alpha-time version is commented out in the source): load controllers at the END of the step, no offset. `math` expressions in BC values read `fem.GetTime().currentTime` (`FEScalarValuator.cpp` l.124) = end of step in both solvers (rotcheck confirmed t exactly, static). Consequences for earlier work (source check only, no new runs): cavity, Hron-Turek CFD1/FSI1 (fluid module, load-curve ramps on `fluid normal velocity`) and the Womersley pilot used rho_inf = 0 (`--rhoi 0` defaults in the generators), so alpha_f = 1 and their ramps were NOT offset; the ring runs used rho_inf = 1 in the fluid-FSI solver with initial-velocity drives only (no time-dependent prescribed values): not affected; the HT CSM body-load ramp was static. Any rho_inf = 1 FLUID-module run with load curves would be offset by dt/2 (at steady state only the ramp end time shifts by dt/2: negligible). Evidence: section 4.2 (wall velocity equals omega(t_n - dt/2) to 7 digits; diagnostic run 14211).
**11.2 Why the coupled Newton iteration stalled.** (a) The solid prescribed rotation is evaluated at t_{n+1} (math BC, FSI InitStep(t)); solid kinematics are Newmark from the displacement (`UpdateKinematics`), so the prescribed displacement and the velocity the fluid sees (v_fluid = v_solid + w) are consistent by construction: no separate prescribed-velocity DOF, no displacement/velocity mismatch (that hypothesis is not supported). (b) Initial state at t = 0 is all zero with S ~ 1e-175: nothing inconsistent; the first four steps are accepted by `min_residual` (A1). (c) The solid domain (standard `elastic-solid` or `three-field-solid`) uses the generalized-alpha configuration F = alpha_f F_t + (1 - alpha_f) F_p, and for alpha_f == 0.5 exactly (rho_inf = 1) it adds an energy-conserving stress correction `s += D ((W_t - W_p)/(dt J) - s:D)/(D:D)` whenever D:D > machine epsilon (`FEElasticSolidDomain.cpp` l.652-671; same condition in `FE3FieldElasticSolidDomain.cpp` l.567). For the NIKE3D Mooney-Rivlin law W = U(J) + dev W with U = K/4 (J^2 - 2 ln J - 1), the increment W_t - W_p at strains of 1e-10 is round-off (J^2 - 2 ln J - 1 cancels to 1e-16 absolute), so the correction is noise divided by D:D, and the Newton tangent does not contain it. The stall began at the step where the rotation rate makes D:D reach epsilon (step 5, 1e-10 rad), is independent of three-field on/off (both domains contain it), of E_mesh, of viscosity and of the outer-mesh clamp, and does not occur for the fluid mixture alone: all as observed. SVK evaluates W from E without cancellation, hence converges. (d) Bulk/shear: K/mu = 2.17 (not near-incompressible); three-field on/off changes only the volumetric treatment and is not the cause. (e) The massless E_mesh skeleton is solved by skeleton elasticity; behaviour independent of E_mesh. (f) Line search (lstol 0.9) reduced the step to 0.5-0.63 in failing iterations (a symptom). (g) A residual slow convergence (4-10 iterations, growing with amplitude) remains for SVK (rho_inf 1) and MR (rho_inf 0.9): UNRESOLVED, consistent with an inexact tangent term (not tested).
