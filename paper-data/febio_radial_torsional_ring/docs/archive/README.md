# Archived working reports

`RTR_PILOT_PLAN.md` is the pre-registration, **byte-identical** to the campaign file (see `../PREREGISTRATION.md` for the hash check). `RTR_EQUIVALENCE_AUDIT.md` and `RTR_PILOT_RESULTS.md` are the campaign's working reports; the only edit is the replacement of a fork account name by `<author-fork>`. They are kept for traceability and are superseded by the curated documents in `docs/` where they differ; the differences are listed in `../../provenance/INCONSISTENCIES.md` (notably the stale outcome paragraph and job table of section 1-2 of the results report, and the wrong drive amplitudes in sections 11.2 and 13). Paths such as `tools/...`, `runs/campaignRTR/...` and `rtr_agent/...` refer to the campaign working tree and are not part of this package.
