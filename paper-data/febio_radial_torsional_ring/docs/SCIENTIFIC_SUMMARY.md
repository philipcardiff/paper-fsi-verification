# Scientific summary: radialTorsionalRing (torsion member) in stock FEBio v4.13

## 1. What was tested, and what it is not
The radialTorsionalRing benchmark is a **draft proposal written by the same author** (manuscript PR #25, solids4foam PR #637; neither is on solids4foam `development`). Its reference is a semi-analytical reduced model written by the same author. The PRs themselves state that no second independent nonlinear implementation has validated it. This package therefore reports an **independent test of the draft proposal's own reference** with a different code lineage (stock FEBio v4.13, hex8 finite elements, monolithic) and an independent fixed-geometry fluid reference. It is **not verification of the merged suite** and does not change any category count of the manuscript.

Only the torsion member (theta0 = 0.1 rad, P0 = 0) was addressed. The radial and combined members were not run.

## 3. Findings
1. **Static solid, VERIFIED AGREEMENT (static only).** FEBio's `Mooney-Rivlin` (c1 = mu/2, c2 = 0, k = K, NIKE3D pressure model) reproduces the proposal's neo-Hookean law: Cauchy stress of a hex8 block under five homogeneous deformations (J down to 0.95) agrees with the hand formula to 1.2e-12 of the maximum stress, and with a finite-difference derivative of the proposal's energy to 3e-10 (limited by the derivative). The `math` rotation boundary condition (C-infinity ramp) reproduces the exact rigid rotation to 7e-13 m. The drive torque of the statically twisted annulus (amplitude 0.5 rad, five times the member's amplitude, finite strain) converges to the proposal's finite-strain reference at observed order 1.95 and 1.99, with max errors 3.4e-3, 8.8e-4, 2.2e-4 of the peak at M1, M2, M3. The dynamic solid was not tested separately.
2. **Fixed-geometry fluid, as run: pre-registered gates FAILED.** Velocity L2 error 1.747 % (M1) and 1.706 % (M2), ratio 1.02 (gate: at most 2 % and ratio of at least 3: the ratio gate fails); shear torque as applied (T2) 12.87 % and 7.47 % of max |T_B| with observed order 0.78 (gates 10 %, 5 %, order 0.8: all fail). Pressure parity (0.09 %, 0.06 %), continuity (1.7e-6, 1.8e-6) and Newton convergence (200/200 steps, 1.86 iterations on average) passed.
3. **The velocity floor is a time-label offset, PROVEN.** In the stock v4.13 *fluid* module a load-curve-driven prescribed fluid velocity is evaluated at t - (1 - alpha_f) dt, which is t - dt/2 at rho_inf = 1; the logged wall velocity equals the table at t - dt/2 to 2.5e-13 rad/s at all 200 steps (source evidence and data in `BC_TIME_CONVENTION.md`). The fluid-FSI solver evaluates load controllers at the end of the step, and `math` expressions read the end-of-step time in both solvers. A single diagnostic run with the table advanced by dt/2 (job 14211) gives the post-hoc aligned numbers directly.
4. **Aligned for the offset (one dt only): QUALIFIED AGREEMENT for velocity, H_f and the reaction torque T3.** Velocity L2 0.186 % and 0.044 % (order 2.07); H_f 0.168 % and 0.046 % (order 1.86); T3 0.26 % and 0.10 % (order 1.35, not clean). This alignment is post hoc and labelled so everywhere; the unaligned as-run gates failed. No time-step study was made, so the temporal part of these errors is not separable and VERIFIED AGREEMENT is not claimed.
5. **Shear torque as FEBio applies it: DIAGNOSTIC DISCREPANCY with a confirmed first-order mechanism.** Aligned errors are 11.42 % and 5.92 % (order 0.95); the first-order estimate made before any run (traction taken from the element-mean viscous stress, at the element centre r = 1 + h/2, evaluated from the exact solution) was 11.7 % and 6.0 %, within 2.4 % and 1.3 % of the observation. FEBio's `fluid-FSI traction` uses the Gauss-point mean of the whole attached element (source: `FEFluidFSITraction::GetFluidStress`), and the swirl shear stress varies strongly across the first cell (decay length about 0.45 m). The label for the mechanism is SUPPORTED, not PROVEN: no run isolates the sampling location, and the agreement rests on an analytical estimate.
6. **Coupled torsion: NOT ESTABLISHED.** With the proposal's Mooney-Rivlin law at rho_inf = 1 the coupled Newton iteration stalls at step 5 of the tiny M0 smoke case (rotation about 1e-10 rad), independent of E_mesh, the three-field switch, the outer-mesh clamp and the fluid viscosity (jobs 14198-14208). The hypothesis that FEBio's energy-conserving stress correction (active only at alpha_f = 0.5 exactly) acts on round-off-level strain-energy differences is SUPPORTED by one diagnostic at rho_inf = 0.9 that removes the stall at M0 (20/20 steps) but missed the pre-registered at most 6 Newton iterations per step (mean 6.95). The coupled M1 run with rho_inf = 0.9 (job 14213) then **diverged at step 2** (squared residual growing by about 1e3 per iteration, 32 negative Jacobians) from a drive of about 3e-24 rad. The cause is UNRESOLVED. RT-C M2 was not run.

## 4. What is claimed and what is not
- Claimed: the static solid law and torsion stiffness agree with the proposal's reference at second order (static only); the fixed-geometry swirl is reproduced to 0.19 % / 0.04 % after correcting a documented time-label offset; FEBio's applied wall shear torque is first-order accurate with a 4-12 % error at the proposal's M1-M2 meshes, in line with a pre-run estimate.
- Not claimed: any verification of the coupled torsion member, of time-step convergence, of K_f independence, or of the merged suite; any validation of the proposal's coupled reference model (its coupled solution was not compared with any FEBio coupled result). What was compared is the static solid reference (proposal's energy) and the fixed-geometry fluid reference (own spectral solver, cross-checked here by a finite-difference solver); FEBio converges towards both within the limits stated above.
- No change to stock FEBio is proposed and no manuscript change is requested by this package.
