# Torque and norm definitions

All torques are the torque of the fluid ON the solid about +z, per radian and per metre of depth: extensive value divided by 2 pi dz, dz = 0.1 m. FEBio logs no reaction force at a fluid-FSI wall, so T1-T3 are **own reconstructions** from the logged nodal fields of the z = 0 layer (current positions, absolute fluid velocity, dilatation, at 12 significant digits). The slab is z-uniform (a top-layer probe line confirms it). They are validated by the exact reference (sign and lever arm: the sign-flipped T2 has E = 190 %), not by FEBio output.

- **T1, face-evaluated viscous torque.** Stress s = 2 mu D - (2 mu/3) tr D I (kappa = 0, as in the input) from the trilinear velocity gradient of the adjacent element evaluated on the wall face (two Gauss points per edge); integrate x cross (-s n_f) dl with n_f the normal out of the fluid. A wall-value traction as a face-based method would apply.
- **T2, the traction as FEBio applies it** (the gated torque). The mean of s over the Gauss points of the whole attached element (centroid value at distance h/2 from the wall; source `FEFluidFSITraction::GetFluidStress`), nodal force f_a = -(s_mean n_f) l dz/4 on each of the four facet nodes, torque = sum (x f_y - y f_x). The pressure part (nodal dilatation) carries no torque for an axisymmetric field and is reported only as a parity diagnostic.
- **T3, angular-momentum (reaction) torque.** T3 = -(dH_f/dt + outer-boundary flux torque), H_f = rho sum (x v_y - y v_x) dV with 2x2 Gauss quadrature of the hex8 elements on the polygon geometry, dH_f/dt by second-order central differences of the logged states. Valid because the rigid-rotation part of the field has zero symmetric gradient, so the viscous and pressure operators cancel (AE). It is the consistent (energy-conjugate) torque, not a load FEBio applies to a structure.
- **Errors.** E_max(q) = max_t |q - q_ref| / max_t |q_ref| over the logged times t_n = n dt, n = 1..200; E_L2 in time by the trapezoid rule. Velocity error: area-weighted L2 over all nodes and times of (v_theta - v_theta_exact, v_r - 0), normalised by the exact v_theta L2 (area weights: ring trapezoid in the campaign script, consistent lumped hex8 weights in the independent script; they agree to 0.03 percentage points). Observed order p = log2(E(M1)/E(M2)).
- **Pressure parity.** a_par = max |p_ij - mean_j p_ij| (theta scatter, absent in the exact solution) and a_rad = max |e_i - (e_{i-1} + e_{i+1})/2| with e_i the theta-mean pressure error, both at the time of maximum exact wall pressure, p = -K_f e_f.
- **Fixed-geometry references.** Spectral (Chebyshev N = 40, Radau 1e-12; `scripts/rtr_reference.py`, N = 40 versus 56 differ by 5e-9) for L = r v_theta with L_t = nu (L_rr - L_r/r) on 1 <= r <= 2, L(1, t) = omega_w(t) (no slip), L_r(2) = L(2) (zero shear stress); T_B = mu (L_r - 2 L) at r = 1. Independent check in `scripts/independent_check.py`: second-order finite differences (6,400 cells), BDF2 with dt = 0.5 ms (Crank-Nicolson was tried first and rejected: it rings at the wall because the tabulated omega has slope jumps every millisecond). Agreement with the spectral reference: 1.3e-5 of max |T_B| and 4e-7 of max |H_f| (the finite-difference solver itself changes by 4e-5 of max under halving of N and dt; this is the stated uncertainty of the cross-check, far below the FEBio errors of 1e-3 and above).
