# Addendum A3b (2026-11-11), registered before use; extends A3 (b)
Finding that motivates it (runs/S/T0, T1; Gc, t = 0.69 -> 0.71): the Newton iteration takes ~13 iterations with the backtracking line search at its lower bound (lambda = 0.1, residual reduced by ~0.9 per iteration for ~10 iterations, then superlinear) and is identical for ksp_rtol 1e-6 and 1e-10: the linear solves are not the cause; the initial guess (previous time level) is too far from the new solution for the strongly nonlinear ALE/solid coupling when the flap moves fast.
