Post-processing/diagnostic only (no change of physics, discretisation, BCs, geometry, mesh): Method force_decomp() evaluates, on the state of a just-solved step (before accept):
 W_all = -F_f(lift_interface) (nodal consistent force, interface nodes incl. clamp-shared), W_free (without clamp-constrained nodes), F_dir (facet traction integral), terms of W_all (time, convection, viscous, pressure),

Assembled-equation identities that must hold to solver tolerance: Fs_all = I - f_b ; I - f_b = R_BC + W_all (free rows of F_f+F_s vanish at the converged state); sum of the four terms = W_all.
Classification rule fixed now: a TRUE action-reaction defect (G) iff either identity fails by > 1e-6 x max(|terms|, 1e-6 N) at converged states.
