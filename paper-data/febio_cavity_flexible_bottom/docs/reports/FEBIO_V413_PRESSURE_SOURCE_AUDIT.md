## 2. The element actually used: `FEFluidFSIDomain3D` (registered "fluid-FSI-3D"; factory `FEFluidFSIDomainFactory.cpp:23` returns it for any solid-element shape)
Unknowns per node (7): solid displacement u (3), relative fluid velocity w (3), fluid dilatation e_f (1). All fluid unknowns use the same nodal shape functions of the (trilinear hex8) element: **equal-order interpolation of w and e_f, 8-point Gauss.** The pressure is not an unknown: p = -K e_f (`FELinearElasticFluid.cpp:57-63`, tangent `dp/de_f = -K` at :67-70; default elastic property of `fluid`, `FEFluid.cpp:65`).
- `Activate()` (l.89-108): every non-excluded fluid node gets w and e_f active (e_f is a free DOF at every node including wall, interface, inlet and outlet nodes until a BC fixes it).
- `UpdateElementStress()` (l.791-882): at each Gauss point `pt.m_ef = el.Evaluate(e,n)` (l.864; e = alpha_f e_t + (1-alpha_f) e_prev, l.823), `Gradef = Gradient(el,e,n)`, `pt.m_gradef = F^-T Gradef` (l.865-866), `pt.m_pf = Pressure(pt.m_ef)` (l.880), viscous stress and fluid acceleration `m_aft = a + aw + Lf w` (l.869). **All pressure quantities at a Gauss point are trilinear interpolations of the nodal e_f.**
- **Momentum residual** `ElementInternalForce` (l.227-307), per node i: `ff = sv*gradN_i + gradp*H_i` (l.292) with `gradp = m_gradef * Tangent_Pressure_Strain` (l.269) = -K grad(e_f). The pressure enters as the **strong-form (non-integrated-by-parts) gradient** `int N_i grad p dV`; there is no boundary pressure integral in the momentum row. Inertia/convection (`m_aft`) is `ElementInertialForce` (l.916-955). Viscous stress: Newtonian, `s = (kappa - 2mu/3) tr(D) I + 2 mu D` (`FENewtonianFluid.cpp:68-80`).
- **Continuity residual** (same function, l.293): `fJ = H_i[(dJf/Jf - dJs/Js) dtrans + (grad e_f . w)/Jf] + gradN_i . w`, i.e. `int N_i e_f-dot/Jf + int gradN_i . w` (the divergence is **integrated by parts**; the boundary term `-oint N_i w.n dA` is absent from the element and is supplied only by `FEFluidNormalVelocity::LoadVector` on the inlet, section 5).
- **Tangent** (`ElementStiffness` l.449-567, `ElementMassMatrix` l.675-757): `kwJ = (svJ gradN_i) H_j + (gradN_j dp + gradef H_j d2p) H_i` (l.529-530), `kJw = (gradef H_i/Jf + gradN_i) H_j` (l.535), `kJJ = ((c - dJfoJ) dtrans - gradef.w/Jf) H_j + gradN_j.w) H_i/Jf` (l.536-537). With `rhoi = 0, order = 2` (`FEFluidFSISolver.cpp:189-201`): alpha_f = 1, alpha_m = 2, gamma = 1.5, beta = 1, so a = gamma/(beta dt) = 0.6, c = alpha_m/(alpha_f gamma dt) = 0.5333 s^-1 at dt = 2.5 s.
- **Consequence (PROVEN algebraically, checked numerically to 1.7e-17 in `b4_operator.py`):** at rest, `kJw = G^T` where `G_ij = int N_i grad N_j` is the block in `kwJ` (times dp = -K). The element is the symmetric-pattern block system `[A G; G^T -(c/K) M]` in the pressure variable p = -K e_f. The only regularisation of the pressure block is the compressibility mass term (c/K) M = 5.3e-7 M.
- **Stabilisation search (PROVEN by source inspection):** `grep -il "stabiliz|pspg|brezzi|supg|upwind"` hits only `FEBackFlowFSIStabilization`, `FETangentialFlowFSIStabilization` (surface loads on outflow boundaries), the solute/PF variants and registration files. **There is no interior pressure stabilisation (no PSPG, SUPG, Brezzi-Pitkaranta, polynomial projection) in `FEFluidFSIDomain3D` or `FEFluidDomain3D`.** `FEFluidFSIDomain3D::m_sseps/m_btrans` only switch the transient terms off for steady analyses. Only one fluid domain class exists, so a compatible (Taylor-Hood-type) velocity/pressure space is not selectable from the input file: e_f always has the element's full nodal space.
- `FEFluidDomain3D::ElementInternalForce` (fluid-only module, used by the C1/C1b runs) has the same structure: `fs = sv*gradN + gradp*H`, `fJ = dJoJ*H + gradN*m_vft` (FEFluidDomain3D.cpp:169-235).

## 3. Interface-row dilatation and the pressure that leaves the solver
- Nodal e_f is the solver unknown and the only thing written as a nodal field (`FEPlotFluidDilatation::Save`, FEBioFluidPlot.cpp:112-123: `node.get(dof_e)`).
- The domain pressure plot `FEPlotFluidPressure::Save` (l.561-571) is `writeAverageElementValue(pt->m_pf)` = **element mean over the 8 Gauss points of -K e_f(xi_g)**. For the hex8 8-point rule the mean of the trilinear interpolant at the 8 points equals the mean of the 8 nodal values, so the plotted element pressure = -K * (mean of the 8 nodal e_f). This is what the audit already verified (xplt element pressure = -K mean nodal ef to 1e-7 Pa). **PROVEN (algebra + data).**
- `FEPlotFluidSurfacePressure::Save` (l.295-323) evaluates `pcs->Evaluate(nface, dof_EF)` on the surface element (nodal average over the facet) then `Pressure(ef)`: a facet-nodal mean. Not in our plotfiles.

## 4. Two force pathways (detail in `FEBIO_FSI_TRACTION_ASSEMBLY_AUDIT.md`)
(1) The load actually applied to the solid: `FEFluidFSITraction::LoadVector` (FEFluidFSITraction.cpp:157-196): p from the **facet's own nodal e_f** (l.121-133, 185-186), 2x2 Gauss, bilinear shape functions, `gt = gr x gs` of the deformed facet (l.175-177), `f = gt p - sv gt` (l.189), `sv` = mean over the adjacent fluid element's Gauss points (l.136-154).
(2) The built-in "fluid surface force" plot (FEBioFluidPlot.cpp:164-226): `-sum_j s_elem * A_j n_j` with `s_elem` = mean of `pt.m_sf` over the adjacent fluid element's Gauss points (pressure from the element, i.e. the 8-node mean, plus the viscous stress). **Pathway (2) sees the row-0/row-1 mean; pathway (1) sees only the interface-row nodal values.** Our runs plot neither the surface force nor the surface pressure.

## 5. Boundary treatments relevant to the diagnosed mode
- `FEFluidNormalVelocity` (inlet): `LoadVector` (l.83-106) adds `int N_i VN da` to the **dilatation row** of the inlet nodes: this is the missing boundary term of the integrated-by-parts divergence for the inlet. Nodal velocity `w = nu*VN` is prescribed in `Update` (l.265-312). With `prescribe_rim_pressure`, rim nodes (boundary nodes whose three velocity DOFs are fixed: the 4 inlet wall corners) get `DOF_PRESCRIBED` e_f (l.252-258) with value = **mean e_f of the non-rim inlet nodes, re-evaluated at every `Update`** (SetRimPressure l.349-372, a lagged constraint). Parabolic profile from `FEParabolicMap` normalised so the discrete mean is 1 (l.316-345).
- Outlet: `zero fluid dilatation` on all outlet nodes removes their continuity rows (p = 0, w free). Walls, interface: `w = 0` removes the momentum rows of those nodes; their continuity rows remain.
- Rows/columns removed per node: a node whose all neighbouring velocity DOFs are fixed keeps a pressure DOF that is coupled to the momentum equations only through its free diagonal neighbours. The two floor-end corners (2,0), (6,0) are such nodes (see `FEBIO_PRESSURE_MODE_ANALYSIS.md` section 3: Schur eigenvalue 3.5e-10 against 2.5e-3 for the next mode).
- `FEFluidPressureLoad`, `FEFluidResidualVector`: not used in any cavity input (the generator uses `zero fluid dilatation`, `prescribed fluid dilatation`, `fluid normal velocity`, `fluid-FSI traction`).

## 6. Code observations (not claimed to be defects; no effect on our model shown)
- `FESurface::LoadVector` (FESurface.cpp:2459-2461) sets `Hr = el.Gr(order,n)` and `Hs = el.Gr(order,n)`: `Hs` re-uses `Gr`. It only affects `dof_a.shape_deriv_s`, which `FEFluidFSITraction::LoadVector` does not use (it uses `dof_a.shape` only). **NOT YET TESTED** whether any other load class depends on it; reported for completeness.
- `FEFluidFSITraction::LoadVector` takes the viscous stress from the adjacent element (Gauss mean) but the pressure from the surface nodes: the two come from different spaces by design (comment l.179-185: "necessarily using the attached solid element ... only from surface element").

## 7. What this audit does NOT establish
Whether the source formulation is "wrong": it is a documented-by-code equal-order Galerkin scheme without interior stabilisation; its behaviour (soft parity pressure modes) is a property of that scheme (see `FEBIO_PRESSURE_MODE_ANALYSIS.md`). No implementation error was found in the paths read: an independent reassembly of the element equations from these source lines reproduces the converged FEBio C1b state to a relative momentum residual of 9.4e-7 and a continuity residual of 2.2e-7 (`results/b4_residual_check.json`) (PROVEN for the fluid-only domain of the fixed-plate run; the FSI domain adds the solid/mesh terms and was not re-assembled).
