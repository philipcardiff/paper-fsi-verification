## 2. Is the pressure boundary-value problem the same?
| Item | solids4foam (contract) | FEBio model (feb read) | Same? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outlet | p = 0 fixed | zero dilatation at all outlet nodes (`outlet_p0`): ef = 0 exactly at 42/42 nodes | yes |
| Inlet | parabolic velocity, mean 1 | `fluid normal velocity`, parabolic, discrete flux 1.0000 | yes (profile 1.0101 x nominal at dx 0.1, O(h^2)) |
| Pressure datum | kinematic p (rho = 1), p = 0 at outlet | p = -K ef (Pa), rho = 1, p = 0 at outlet | yes (rho = 1 so kinematic = Pa) |
| Plate underside | traction free | traction free (no load on the bottom face) | yes |
| Compressibility | incompressible | K = 1e6; K x10 changes u_y by 1e-6 and Fy by 1.4e-6 (C4) | yes within 1e-6 |
| **Extra constraint** | none | 4 floor-end corner nodes pinned to p = 1.15 Pa (value taken from FEBio's own C1 floor mean) | **no, an added constraint** |
| Interface kinematics | no-slip conformal | no-slip, shared nodes | yes |
| Solid | StVK plane strain | StVK hex8, 3-D slab with u_z = 0 | yes (hex8 stiffer: S1) |
The pin is not a benchmark condition and it is also not a datum shift (the outlet fixes the datum). In M2 the pinned values 1.15 are below their neighbours (left 1.2045, 1.1719; right 1.3895, 1.3616). Bound (not a correction): replacing the pinned values by linear extrapolation changes Fy by -0.0096 N/m (0.19 %) for M2 and -0.0194 (0.41 %) for C2 (and makes the force **larger in magnitude**, i.e. it would close at most about 0.2-0.4 percentage points of the 5.6-7.9 % deficit). The pressure level is determined by the outlet datum, so a "gauge constant" explanation is excluded: p = 0 at the outlet is imposed identically in both codes.

## 3. Pressure in the channel (M2/C2, slab-averaged to remove the y checkerboard)
- Outlet p = 0, inlet mean p = 1.4298 (M2) / 1.4040 (C2), inlet-to-outlet drop 1.43 Pa vs plane-Poiseuille 1.68 Pa over 14 m (the cavity region is not Poiseuille; this is not a defect by itself).
- dp/dx: upstream x 0.3-1.7: -0.1237 (M2), -0.1248 (C2), expectation -0.12121 (the pair-averaged value is within 2-3 %); above the cavity x 2.3-5.7: +0.0015 / +0.0036 (flat); downstream x 6.3-13.7: -0.1249 / -0.1216, with a local slope about -0.139 at x = 7-8 relaxing to -0.119 at x = 12-13.
- The floor pressure is therefore set by the downstream drop from the cavity exit to p = 0 at x = 14 plus the cavity-exit loss: it increases with refinement: interface mean 1.178 (M1) -> 1.232 (M2) Pa; Fy -4.718 -> -4.934; deficit vs QM -7.9 % -> -5.6 %. A second-order extrapolation is not warranted from two levels with a hex8/odd-even component; M3 is needed.
- The y checkerboard in the channel (for example 1.192/1.613 alternating at x about 1) is large in the channel but the interface amplitude falls from 0.0708 Pa (M1) to 0.0076 Pa (M2): odd-even bracket on Fy +/-3.0 % -> +/-0.4 %.

## 4. Attribution of the force difference (classes A-F; what the evidence supports)
| Class | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A different pressure BC | outlet and datum identical; only the 4-node pin differs (0.19-0.41 %, wrong sign to explain the deficit) | **excluded as the main cause**; a 0.2-0.4 % contribution remains |
| B odd-even oscillations | bracket +/-3.0 % (M1) -> +/-0.4 % (M2); the deficit is 7.9 -> 5.6 % and one-sided | **not the main cause at M2**; limits M1 |
| C fluid discretisation (resolution) | floor pressure and Fy rise with dx (M1 -> M2 -> M3: mean 1.178 -> 1.232 -> 1.245 Pa; Fy -4.718 -> -4.934 -> -4.983), but the deficit flattens (-7.9, -5.6, -5.1 %; extrapolated about -5 %) | **a resolution error alone is NOT supported**: the M2->M3 step is 4x smaller and the extrapolation stays about 5 % below -5.26; an equal-order-scheme error that does not vanish with dx is not excluded |
| D force integration | pressure-only, viscous 2-pt and total are reproduced from the fields; orientation, TZ division checked | **excluded** at 0.025 % (viscous); FEBio-reaction check unavailable |
| E pressure transfer to the plate | S2: plate under the interface pressure reproduces u_y(A) to 0.025 % (pressure traction only) | **excluded for the vertical pressure load**; viscous/total traction transfer not tested |
| F physical-model difference | weak compressibility excluded (K x10: 1e-6); material law is StVK in both; plane-strain slab; hex8 plate stiffness already accounted (S1) | **not excluded**: the solid constitutive/element difference, the no-slip/viscous treatment on a conformal weakly compressible equal-order hex8 scheme, and the cavity-exit loss are untested |

- **What is settled (supported by evidence):** the vertical pressure force that FEBio puts on the plate is about 5 % below the solids4foam value at every mesh level, converged in dx to within about +/-1 %; the BCs that the two models share (outlet p = 0, no underside load, rho, no-slip, inlet flux) are identical; the corner pin explains at most 0.2-0.4 %; viscous Fy explains 0.03 %; the load transfer (S2) and reconstruction (D) are fine.
