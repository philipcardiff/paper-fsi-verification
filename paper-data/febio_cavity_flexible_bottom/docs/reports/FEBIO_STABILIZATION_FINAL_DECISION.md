## Why the correction failed, precisely
1. The term works as designed on the diagnosed mode (element-level energy exact; D reduced 4500-fold; independent re-assembly of the solved equations closes: residual with the term 1.7e-7).
2. Its consistency error is not small at this resolution: (p - Pi p) = O(h |grad p|), the weak consistency is O(h^2) but with a first-order-in-alpha, O(1)-constant coefficient; with alpha = 3/4 (the Schur-complement bound of the actual kappa = 0 viscous law) and h/H = 0.1 it changes the smooth pressure by 3-7 % and, through boundary rows with essential conditions, the flux by up to 2 %. The straight-channel test against the exact Poiseuille solution shows that the interior profile and dp/dx are in fact closer to exact (error 1.0 % -> 0.26 %, 0.98 % -> 0.21 %) while the flux is 0.7 % worse: the method trades interior accuracy for a boundary mass defect at this mesh.
3. A second, unforeseen interaction: the inlet rim-pressure constraint is lagged in the stock Jacobian; with the term the Newton iteration converged only linearly (43 iterations per step). A solver-side remedy (rim updated once per time step when alpha > 0) was added and verified (quadratic convergence again); it does not change the converged state.
No alpha other than the predeclared 3/4 was run; the proportionality of the defect to alpha and (h/H)^2 is a derivation-based prediction, NOT TESTED.

## What this does and does not say about the benchmark and FEBio
* The diagnosed unstabilised-pair mode (class B of `FEBIO_PRESSURE_MODE_ANALYSIS.md`) is now removable by a stated weak-form term: its role in the F1 - F2 split is SUPPORTED by the 0.8196 -> 0.0054 N/m change. It does NOT show that the parity-free value is the true force: F2 changed by 6.3 % when the parity was removed, so F2 (the plot-pathway average) was not a faithful estimate of the stabilised smooth field either.
* No claim of a verified source correction is made (full validation requirements not satisfied: conservation failed, no coupled steady run, no convergence study, single mesh and alpha).
* No solids4foam value or -5.25 N/m was used for any choice.
