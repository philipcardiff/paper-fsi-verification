# CAVITY C3: coupled FEBio mesh study (CAVITY agent)
## PRE-REGISTRATION (before the runs)
Levels (conformal hex8 fluid + hex8 StVK plate, same generator/physics/numerics as the pilot C2; only the in-plane divisions change, factor f doubles every block division and the plate cells): **M1 = C2 (dx 0.1, 1688 + 160 el, run already made)**, M2 = `C3_fsi_M2` (dx 0.05, 6752 + 640), M3 = `C3_fsi_M4` (dx 0.025, 27008 + 2560; the directory suffix is the mesh-factor 4). Numerics identical (K 1e6, rhoi 0, dt 2.5 s, ramp 25 s, E_mesh 1e-3, corner pin 1.15 Pa, tolerances); t_end 900 s (not 1250: C2 terminated on the dilatation floor at t = 975 s although steady at about 700 s). 4 and 8 cores (see ledger). M4 (dx 0.0125, 108032 fluid cells) is NOT planned: the cost (about 4 x M3) and the unresolved M1 force deficit do not justify it (brief: conditional on demonstrated need and expected improvement).
Questions to be answered by the data (no tuning): (i) does u_y(A) converge and to what value, (ii) does F_y(pressure) converge and does the 8 % deficit against solids4foam persist, (iii) does the odd-even bracket shrink with dx, (iv) cost. Observed orders only with three levels outside the noise: M1-M3 differences; note M1 includes the hex8 stiffening (S1: 4.5 %, 1.3 %, 0.3 % at 40x4, 80x8, 160x16).

All three terminated on the dilatation-increment floor (M3: dilatation 6.2e-30 vs required 3.9e-31, all other norms at 1e-23..1e-24); the analysed states are the last logged ones. **M3 was still relaxing slowly** (u_y(A) range -0.23016 .. -0.22997 over t in [650,717], drift about 1e-4 m per 50 s), so its steady value carries an uncertainty of about +/-2e-4 m (+/-1e-3 relative); M3 is a valid but not fully steady result. Mass imbalance at the outlet: +1.2e-4, -1.9e-5, -2.3e-5.
Differences between levels: u_y -0.014266, -0.003805 (ratio 3.75, **observed order 1.91**); F_y -0.2163, -0.0496 (ratio 4.36, **observed order 2.13**). Richardson estimates (indicative only: three levels, M3 not fully steady, M1 includes hex8 plate stiffening of 4.5 %): u_y -> **-0.2314 m** (p observed -0.23142; p = 2 -0.23131), F_y -> **-5.00 N/m** (p observed -4.998; p = 2 -5.000). If M3 is taken at the other end of its drift range the u_y order changes by about +/-0.1 and the extrapolation by about +/-0.0003 m.
Comparison at the same dx with solids4foam: u_y vs partitioned **+3.6 %, -2.2 %, -3.5 %**; F_y vs QM **-7.9 %, -5.6 %, -5.1 %**; extrapolations vs the solids4foam extrapolations (-0.2407 m, -5.26 N/m): **-3.9 % and -4.9 %**.
Findings:
1. **The FEBio steady force does NOT converge to the published value**: it converges (order about 2) towards -5.00 N/m, about 5 % below solids4foam (-5.26), Tukovic (-5.257), ANSYS (-5.27) and COMSOL (-5.30), which agree with each other to 0.2-0.7 %. The displacement is consistently about 4 % smaller in magnitude than the solids4foam value at the finest levels.
2. The displacement/force ratio is consistent with the solids4foam plate: FEBio |u_y|/|F_y| = 0.2314/5.00 = 0.0463 m per N/m, solids4foam 0.2407/5.26 = 0.0458 (1 % apart, plausible with the load distribution and StVK nonlinearity); and S2 shows that the FEBio plate reproduces the coupled deflection under the interface pressure to 0.03 %. **The structural response and the load transfer are therefore consistent; the discrepancy is in the pressure load that FEBio's fluid delivers to the plate (about 5 %).** The cause is NOT identified. Not the bulk modulus (K x10: 1e-6 at M1), not the pseudo-time step (dt/2: 2e-5), not the nonlinear tolerances (identical), not the odd-even mode (bracket +/-0.4-3 %, shrinking only to +/-1 % at M3), not the plate (S1/S2). Untested candidates: the corner pressure pin (1.15 Pa; 0.2-0.4 % estimated at M1, untested at M2/M3 where the pinned value differs from the local neighbour by a different amount), the zero-dilatation outlet (natural velocity condition) versus the s4f zero-gradient outlet, the inlet profile (nodal parabola with 1.0101 x nominal peak; flux exact; O(h^2)), unstabilised Galerkin convection, E_mesh (1e-3) and rhoi, and a genuine difference in how FEBio's fluid-FSI traction defines the pressure (S2 shows internal consistency only).
3. The odd-even mode amplitude does not decrease monotonically (0.071, 0.0076, 0.024 Pa); the +/-1-3 % force uncertainty it implies is not a convergent quantity at these levels.
4. Cost: M1 1.0 core-h, M2 4.4, M3 28.2 (about 6.4x per refinement at 4x the cells, 8 cores for M3); a fourth level (dx = 0.0125, 108032 fluid cells) would be about 4-5x M3 (>120 core-h, ~14 GB) and is **not run**: the unexplained force deficit, not the mesh, limits the comparison.
