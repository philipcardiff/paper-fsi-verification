# CAVITY ROW-PARITY PRESSURE MODE: coordinator plausibility check (existing data only, no simulation), 2026-10-30
Data: `cavity_agent/b2/ybranch_M3_t717p5.json` (agent's extraction from the C3_fsi_M4 xplt, nodal p = -K ef, interior floor nodes, z = 0 layer; first-row spacing h_y = 0.02679 m).
- Mean nodal pressure by row above the floor (rows 0..5): 1.24513, 1.37869, 1.24536, 1.37882, 1.24544, 1.37890 Pa. The even rows (0, 2, 4) and the odd rows (1, 3, 5) form two flat branches **separated by 0.1335 Pa that does not decay with distance from the wall through six rows**; row2 - row1 = -0.1333.
- Physical scale: a pressure step of 0.1335 Pa over one row (0.027 m) is a wall-normal gradient of about 5.0 Pa/m, 40 times the streamwise gradient of the whole channel (0.12 Pa/m), alternating in sign from row to row. Next to a flat no-slip wall the wall-normal momentum balance gives dp/dy = mu d2v/dy2 (v = O(y^2)); with Re = 100 flow of speed O(1) m/s that is orders of magnitude below 5 Pa/m (order-of-magnitude argument, not computed from the field here). A real pressure does not alternate at constant amplitude every row.
- Consequence if the alternation is a numerical row-parity mode of the equal-order hex8 scheme: the wall (even) row is on the low branch, each branch is wrong by about D/2 = 0.067 Pa (5 % of 1.245), and the best unbiased estimate of the smooth pressure is the pair mean. The integrated floor force with the pair mean is -5.2494 N/m (diagnostic only, not applied) against the raw -4.9835 and the solids4foam values -5.2504 (QM, same dx), Tukovic/ANSYS about -5.25 (agent's table). The 1-2-1 average of rows 0-2 gives -5.2500.
- **Status:** hypothesis, now with a physical-scale argument and a clean data signature, still found after the gap was known and with an unknown mechanism; it is not applied to any reported FEBio number. Alternative explanations that would produce the same data (a genuine physical gradient, or a discrete boundary condition difference at the wall) are not excluded by this check. A test that would settle it needs new output (the full nodal pressure on the wall-normal rows at several x in a fixed-geometry run, or the solids4foam floor pressure), not a new matrix. No run is authorised.
