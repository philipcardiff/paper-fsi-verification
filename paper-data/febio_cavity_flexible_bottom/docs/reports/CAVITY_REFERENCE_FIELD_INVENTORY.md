## 4. What is missing, precisely (needed for a local comparison)
For solids4foam M2 (dx 0.05, 6752 fluid / 640 solid cells), steady (t = 200 at dt 0.5, or the QM t = 300): (1) fluid p and U at the written times (cell-centred) and the boundary values on `interface`; (2) `interface` patch face centres/areas on the deformed mesh (deformed geometry); (3) wall shear stress on the interface and total viscous force (`forces` already logs pressure and viscous separately in `force.dat`; the repository only keeps the sum); (4) Fx (the verification CSV keeps Fy only); (5) `phi` flux through inlet and outlet; (6) the force/point-A time histories (steady-state proof; `postProcessing/fluid/forces/0/force.dat`, `pointDisp`). All are one OpenFOAM run away (proposal in `cavity_agent/proposal_s4f_M2/`, not run).

## 5. Observations relevant to the force question that follow from retrieving the data
1. The force reference is not solids4foam alone: Tukovic (own FV code, published), ANSYS (FE, structured quad mesh, 3-D slab with symmetry) and COMSOL agree on F_y at the same dx to 0.2 % (M2: -5.229, -5.218, -5.2276 vs QM -5.2266; COMSOL +0.7 % at dx <= 0.025). F_y is therefore supported by three codes written by different groups, after depth conversions confirmed by their authors. This weakens the possibility that the solids4foam force is the outlier.
2. ANSYS and COMSOL report Fx: ANSYS -0.01626/-0.01641/-0.01639/-0.01741 N/m (dx 0.1..0.0125, x100), COMSOL -0.0105/+0.0131/-0.0143/-0.0143 (sign flips at dx 0.05: COMSOL Fx is not converged). The sign convention of their Fx is not stated; FEBio's total Fx (pressure + viscous) is +0.0017/-0.0025/-0.0039 (M1-M3). This is a 0.01-0.015 N/m difference, 0.3 % of F_y, and its meaning is open.
3. The scalar tables show that u_x(A) of FEBio (3.3e-4) is 3-4 times that of solids4foam (9e-5) and ANSYS (8e-5) and 2-3 times COMSOL (1.2-1.7e-4); this is a load-asymmetry indicator (CAVITY_SOLVER_FIELD_COMPARISON.md sec. 4).
