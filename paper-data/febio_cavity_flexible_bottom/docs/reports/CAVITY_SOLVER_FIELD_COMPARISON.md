2. **New finding (existing FEBio data, not previously audited): the nodal pressure in the cavity carries a row-to-row (y-parity) alternation, and the interface row sits on its low branch.** Branch separation D = p(row 1) - p(row 0) = 0.363 / 0.171 / 0.134 Pa at M1/M2/M3 (about 30 %, 14 %, 11 % of the mean floor pressure). The force obtained from FEBio's own raw interface row is -4.718/-4.934/-4.983; the mean of the first two nodal rows (equal to FEBio's own element pressure of the wall-adjacent elements) gives **-5.437/-5.275/-5.249** against solids4foam QM -5.1226/-5.2266/-5.2504, Tukovic -5.160/-5.229/-5.2494, ANSYS -5.144/-5.218/-5.250: **-0.02 % at M3, +0.9 % at M2, +6 % at M1** (QM). 3. The earlier-listed uncertainties are small relative to the gap: pin 0.10 % (M3), x-parity bracket +/-1.0 % (M3), M3 incomplete steady state 0.007 % (F_y) and 0.04 % (u_y), viscous 0.03 %. Even taking all of them in the favourable direction the raw force stays 4.0 % (M3) to 5.0 % (M2) below the references (sec. 6).
4. The pressure datum is equivalent in the two solvers (outlet p = 0, free underside), sec. 7.
5. A different deformation is not the cause of the load gap: the floor deflection difference (0.005-0.008 m) changes F_y by at most about 0.04 % (sec. 8).
6. Momentum balance (B3): the vertical interface force inferred from the cavity control volume agrees with the direct integral to 0.03-0.08 % (M3, M2) and the whole-domain balance to 0.2 %; but the check is blind to the y-parity alias (sec. 3), and the horizontal balance is not usable (residual 0.09-0.14 N/m against F_x of 0.002-0.02).

## 1. Common coordinate and deformed geometry
Coordinate used: the **undeformed x of the floor material point (x_ref in [2,6])** for p(x) and tractions, because both solvers keep the plate ends clamped (x = 2, 6) and every floor point maps to a unique deformed point; integrals are taken along each curve's own deformed polyline. Arc length of the deformed curve (normalised) is the alternative (`--coord arc`); it differs from x_ref by at most the slope-dependent stretch (max slope 0.157-0.167, so the arc-length factor deviates from 1 by < 1.4 % locally). ## 3. B3: independent force check by momentum balance

Reading:
- The vertical balance is satisfied to 0.03-0.5 % **except for the M1 cavity box**. There the mouth line y = 0.75 contains 7 rows above the floor, i.e. an odd row index, so the mouth nodes lie on the *high* branch of the y-parity alternation (mouth mean p 1.54 Pa, floor row 1.18 Pa) while at M2/M3 (14 and 28 rows, even index) floor and mouth lie on the *same* branch. The mouth force -6.168 vs floor -4.718 differs by 31 % for a nearly stagnant cavity where p should be uniform in y to about 1 %. This is a direct, internal demonstration that raw nodal wall/line pressure in FEBio is a parity-dependent quantity at M1; at M2/M3 the balance passes only because the two lines share a parity. A balance that closes to 0.08 % therefore does **not** validate the force level; it validates internal consistency of one branch.
- The whole-domain y balance (wall rows are even rows: floor y = 0, channel walls y = 1 and 2) also closes only within a branch.
- Horizontal balance: residual +0.117 (M2), +0.136 (M3), +0.089 (M1) N/m whole domain, +0.054 (M2) cavity; these are 4-8 % of the 1.43 Pa x 1 m inlet pressure force and 5-60 times the plate's own F_x (0.002-0.022). Dominant uncertainty: wall viscous force from element-average velocities (1-pt vs 2-pt differ by 0.012 on the top wall and 0.009 on the downstream wall) and first-order open-boundary viscous terms, plus the difference between a pointwise post-processed traction and the consistent weak-form nodal force. The balance cannot test F_x or the viscous interface force.
- Uncertainty of the vertical check: element-average velocity only (viscous F_y 0.001-0.006 N/m, 0.02-0.1 % of F_y); pressure only through the nodal values (x-parity amplitude 0.0076/0.0237 Pa cancels in the edge average to within one half-edge at each end, about 0.001 N/m); the M3 state at t = 500 is not fully steady (F_y moves 0.0026 between 500 and 717.5).
- **Extra output needed** to make the balance independent of this post-processing (not requested to be run): (i) nodal `wx, wy, ef` for all z = 0 nodes at the last two accepted steps (Mon_all as in C2: about 7.7k x 3 numbers at M2, a few MB) so that wall gradients use nodes, not element averages; (ii) FEBio's fluid stress tensor or surface traction output per interface face (not in the present xplt dictionary, which has only displacement, dilatation, reaction forces, element pressure, element relative velocity), which would give the load FEBio actually passes to the plate; (iii) a working reaction-force output on the plate clamps (currently identically zero: cause not established).

(`cavity_agent/b2/ybranch_*.json`). The alternation is a clean row-parity pattern through the first six rows; the floor row, the second row above it and all even rows share the low branch. Properties:
- Reproducible and deterministic: identical to 4-5 digits for dt/2, K x10 and tolerance changes (M1), present in the fixed-plate fluid-only run (so it is a fluid-discretisation property independent of coupling), and **absent in a straight channel**: the Poiseuille run (`runs/cavity/B0_poiseuille_normalvel`, 140x10 cells) has y-parity 0.0012 Pa and pair-mean pressure within 0.002 Pa of the analytic line. - The pair mean of rows 0 and 1, integrated facet by facet, equals by construction the integral of FEBio's own element pressure of the first element row (the "fluid pressure" element value is the mean of the element's nodal values, verified to 1e-7 Pa in the audit), i.e. it is a FEBio output variable, not an external filter; row 1 alone (high branch) would give -5.62 (M2), -5.52 (M3).
- Plate-only Q9 (20x2) under the pair-mean M3 load: u_y(A) -0.23768, u_x(A) 1.6e-4; M2 load: -0.23917, 2.3e-4 (`cavity_agent/b2/plate_q9_pairmean_M{2,3}.json`); with the 20x2 -> 40x4 stiffness correction (0.67 %, PR #20) M3 -> about -0.2393 vs solids4foam -0.2382/-0.2384, i.e. +0.5 %; raw load: -0.22866, 3.8e-4. Three independent observables (F_y, u_y(A), u_x(A)) move towards the references together at M3.
- The earlier gate record already diagnosed an aliasing defect in criterion A4 (raw upstream dp/dx +60 %, pair-averaged within 0.3 %); the present result extends that diagnosis from the channel to the **wall row that carries the load**. The earlier "odd-even amplitude" and "+/-1 %" brackets refer to x-parity along the interface (0.0076-0.0708 Pa); the y-parity offset is 3-17 times larger and was not previously audited.

Hypothesis (labelled): FEBio's equal-order mixed hex8 formulation carries an unforced row-parity pressure mode in the cavity; the wall nodes lie on its low branch, so the pressure FEBio transmits to the plate is lower than the smooth pressure by about D/2 (0.067 Pa at M3, 5.1 % of the mean). Against it: (a) it was identified after the gap was known (confirmation-bias risk; the check against four independent references at M3 is a post-hoc fit with no free parameter, but at M1 it overshoots by 6 % and at M2 by 0.9 %); (b) the pair mean is a plausible but not proven estimator of the smooth part when D is 30 % of p (M1); (c) the mechanism is not identified (not investigated: no FEBio source change or run was allowed); (d) the solids4foam pressure field has not been seen, so a different cause (e.g. a flow-rate difference) that coincides in size cannot be excluded. What would settle it: solids4foam M2 floor p(x) (proposal) should equal FEBio's M2 pair mean (1.2324+1.4037)/2 = 1.318 Pa (mean over x) and not the raw 1.232 Pa; a floor p(x) at 1.23 would refute it.

## 7. Pressure datum
FEBio: ef = 0 exactly at every outlet node (zero fluid dilatation, M1-M3: `outlet.max_abs_ef = 0`), no load on the underside (feb `<Loads>`), p = -K e_f in Pa with rho = 1. solids4foam: `0/fluid/p` outlet fixedValue 0 (kinematic), `0/solid/D` bottom traction 0, pressure 0, forces `rhoInf 1`. Therefore p = 0 at x = 14 and zero external pressure on the underside in both: **the datum is equivalent**. The mean floor pressure is set by the hydraulic drop from x = 14 (Poiseuille -0.12 Pa/m downstream plus the cavity-exit region), not by a gauge constant. FEBio's mean floor pressure (raw) 1.245 Pa (M3) vs F_y/4 of solids4foam 1.3126: difference 0.068 Pa = 0.57 m of channel Poiseuille drop (or 5 % of the flow rate); the pair-mean 1.312 matches.

## 8. Cause vs consequence (deformation)
The floor shape differs (u_y(A) -3.5 %, M3). Effect of the deformation on the load: C1b (fixed plate, M1) gave raw F_y -4.6637 and mean-row -5.4833; the deformed M1 gave -4.7176 and -5.4375; change +0.054 and -0.046 for a 0.212 m deflection, i.e. |dF_y/du_y| <= 0.26 N/m per m for both definitions (within the x-parity bracket). A deflection difference of 0.0082 m (M3) then changes F_y by at most 0.002 N/m (0.04 %), and 0.0048 m (M2) by 0.001 N/m. **The 5 % load difference is not a consequence of the different deformation**, and the u_y difference is a consequence of the load difference (sec. 2). An equal-deflection comparison of local fields needs the solids4foam profile and cannot be done now; the plate-only route (S2/Q9) was used instead to map a load to a deflection (sec. 2).
