# collapsibleChannel: same-lineage cross-code check (FEniCSx, monolithic ALE)

Status: **draft supplementary evidence. A cross-code check by the same research group and tooling lineage as the reference runs; not an independent validation, not externally reviewed. The solver repository is private and the raw run output is not yet public: this package does not allow the study to be reproduced from the PR alone (only the derived tables and figures can be regenerated from the input CSVs, see below) until an archived snapshot exists.**
Text: `../../supplement_fenicsx_collapsiblechannel.tex` (build alone with `latexmk -pdf supplement_fenicsx_collapsiblechannel.tex`; 9 pages including references; it cites `Baratta2023` (DOLFINx), added to `bibliography.bib` byte-identically to the open draft PRs #69 and #70 (merge-tested in all orders), plus `PETSc`, `MUMPS:1`, `MUMPS:2`, `Heil2006` already in main).
Layout follows `paper-data/fenicsx_cavityflexiblebottom/` (PR #70) and `fenicsx_blobintreacle/` (PR #69). Related collapsibleChannel work in the paper: PR #30 (FEBio audit, open draft), PR #45 (strict-tolerance sweeps, merged), PR #51 (mesh-study figure and the solids4foam F1-F4 data, merged), `paper-data/benchmark_inventory.md`.

## Reproduce the derived tables and figures
Use `python3 -B -I` so that no `__pycache__` is left behind (also ignored by `.gitignore`).
```
cd paper-data/fenicsx_collapsiblechannel
python3 -B -I scripts/analyze_collapsible.py   # derived CSVs, byte-identical to the committed ones
python3 -B -I scripts/make_figures.py          # needs matplotlib; PDFs byte-identical, PNGs not guaranteed across matplotlib builds
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
# optional, needs the evidence tarball (not in git; see below):
python3 -B -I scripts/extract_from_raw.py PATH/TO/collapsible_evidence data
```

## Review lineage
Code written and run by AI agents of one model family for the same research group: COL-1 (structural preflight), COL-2 (fluid preflight and beam model-form probe), COL-3 (coupled solver, P2-triangle wall gates, production preregistration draft), a coordinator step that hashed and froze the production preregistration, COL-P (production matrix, stage 3, MF1 and the results note), COL-A (internal audit; implemented nothing in the campaign; verdict **QUALIFIED**, `audit/AUDIT_INTERNAL_AI_A.md`, with its own rerun job 16937 reproducing the S1 dt 0.025 cell at 6.5e-15 m from a fresh archive of df24818), and the Track A zero-compute audits and the temporal audit that designed the campaign (internal AI audits of the same lineage). No human outside the lineage and no external group has reviewed any of it. The audit's required wording restrictions, applied in the supplement: cross-code consistency check by a code of the same lineage, never "independent validation"; U_ref (0.30 % of P) and the unresolved beam-versus-continuum model form quoted with every agreement statement; the 0.5 % floor attributed neither to solids4foam nor to oomph-lib; no spatial order and no spatial limit; time limits only with the p-sensitivity band; H2 supported at three rows and marginal at the last; solids4foam F1-F4 marked pre-#671; FM-W, WL3 not run; S3 window t <= 1.25 s; P = 0.216 m and the percent-of-peak definition explicit; the "time-corrected s4f range" not quoted.

## Differences between this package and the campaign's results note
All numbers of the supplement were re-extracted from the raw JSON and agree with the note and the audit, with these differences:
- Primary criterion S1 at dt 0.00625: recomputed 1.4645 (note: 1.47; rounding). All other values of the criterion agree.
- H2 last row: the note and the audit use the oomph deficits quoted in the preregistration (1.40/0.375/0.125/0.019) and obtain 0.051 (fail); the deficits recomputed from the reference CSV (1.4006/0.3775/0.1248/0.0207) give 0.0494 (pass by 0.0006). Both are shown; the audit's wording is kept ("fails as written").
- The p-sensitivity band quoted in the audit (-100.17 to -100.23 at x = 10) is that of S2; S3 gives -100.18 to -100.25 (`time_limit_p_sensitivity.csv`).
- The "3-6 times smaller" ratio of the open-loop gap to the FE-minus-oomph offset holds at x = 10 only (0.033 against 0.15-0.20 gives 4.6-6); at the quarter points the offsets are 0.05-0.11 % P (midplane-based -0.08 to -0.12) and the gap 0.053-0.056, ratio about 0.9-2 (at x = 12.5 the offset can be smaller than the gap); only the sign is compared there; the sign is opposite at all three points. The MF1 beam gap grows on refinement (80 elements 0.016-0.022, 160 elements 0.033-0.056; 320 not run): it is not a bound.
- The FE-minus-oomph same-dt offset is "constant at about -0.15 % P" at x = 10 (S2 -0.147 to -0.161, S3 -0.169 to -0.179); at x = 7.5 and 12.5 it is -0.05 to -0.11.
- Same-dt history L-inf differences to oomph of 0.3-0.6 % P apply to S2 and S3; S1 gives 0.7-1.3.

- Error-series correlation 0.85 is for S3 only (S2: 0.79; 0.83 and 0.75 over 1.5 s).
- The H2 row at dt 0.00625 passes at the observed order only by 0.004 and fails at p_ext = 1 (0.077); rows 1-2 pass at every exponent tested. The S2 to S3 history changes (0.09-0.11 % P) are at the lower edge of the registered 0.1-0.5 band, not inside it.
- Job counts: sacct and `cells.csv` give 26 production jobs (25 cells plus the cancelled 16869; 11.03 CPU-h), whereas the shipped erratum text (`audit/ERRATUM_AND_MF1_ADDENDUM.md`, verbatim from the results note) says "27 jobs". The campaign worktree files were not edited.
- The oomph "reference" is a regenerated oomph-lib solution of the tutorial's modified problem (no pre-stress, h/a = 1/20, clamped, ramp, regularising density with a 0.28 % P shift), not a published table, produced by the same group; U_ref 0.30 % P is the tutorial's own estimate.

## Status of the findings (not preregistered versus registered)
Registered outcomes: H1 falsified; H2 passes three rows and fails the last as written; H2x additive but vacuous; H3 not evaluable; H4a, H4c, H5, H7, H8 pass; H4b untested. Not preregistered (introduced at analysis or audit stage, exploratory): the successive-difference comparison with oomph-lib, the constancy of the offset in dt, the p_ext band, the history-level cross term and H1 reading, the solids4foam window statistics and the error correlation. MF1 was preregistered (AMEND2) after the production results and the offset were known. After excluding these, what survives as registered is: the solver runs, its gates pass, H1 failed, H2 is marginal.

## What is not claimed
No independence; no agreement with an external reference beyond the qualifier; no spatial order or limit; no cause of the 0.5 % floor or of the trough offset; no closed-loop model-form equivalence (open loop only, one cell); no statement about FEBio's discretisation; no estimate of the #671 effect for the Robin dt 0.00625 production baseline over the full run; S1 is not converged in the history norm; the FM-W route, WL3 and the conditional S4 level were not run; no change of the benchmark's classification (category B) is proposed.

## Known open points
Tied wall/fluid mesh family; one cell for MF1; the oomph spatial-error estimate (0.26 % P: refinement 1 against 2, history L-inf at dt 0.025, sign unknown) is not removed; the oomph reference was not rerun; the "fresh-environment" S1 rerun used the same conda environment; solids4foam F1-F4 are one run per level; no human review outside the lineage; the solver repository and raw data are not public.
