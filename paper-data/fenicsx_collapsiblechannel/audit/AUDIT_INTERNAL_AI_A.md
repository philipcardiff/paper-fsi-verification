Why the spatial V1 series is non-monotone (E, quantified where possible):
1. Not interpolation or output sampling (above).
2. The history is NOT non-monotone in space. L-inf over t <= 1.5 of (S2-S1) and (S3-S2) at dt 0.00625: x=7.5 0.66 / 0.09, x=10 0.99 / 0.11, x=12.5 0.73 / 0.10% P (ratios 7-9, L2 ratios about 7.5, i.e. order about 2.8-3.2; same at dt 0.0125); both located at t = 1.3-1.5. This is a convergent, roughly third-order series, consistent with the V2/V3 p = 3.3-3.8 that the report calls unexplained. The V1 trough is a stationary value, first-order insensitive to the phase error that carries the spatial error, so spatial effects there are tiny and sit at the level where the wall-ladder signature dominates.
3. Mesh-family confound (E): the wall x-resolution is tied to the fluid (nx_wall = 10 ny, rows fixed at 4), so S1->S3 changes fluid h, wall nx and wall aspect ratio together. The wall-only static ladder (COL3_RESULTS) is monotone with p = 1.27: mid deflection 80x4/160x4/320x4 = -0.14353/-0.14363/-0.14367 m, i.e. deeper by 0.046 then 0.019% P (static scale); scaled to trough depth (x 1.5, E) 0.07 then 0.03, versus observed V1 S2->S3 -0.018..-0.026 (same sign and size) and S1->S2 +0.013..+0.045 (opposite sign: a fluid contribution of about +0.1 at ny 8 -> 16, E). So the pattern "wall nx error (deeper, monotone) plus fluid mesh error (shallower, large only at ny = 8)" reproduces the non-monotone V1 without any inconsistency. It cannot be demonstrated here because WL3 (decoupling wall nx) was not run. Wall rows 2/8 (stage 3) move V1 by 0.005/-0.001% P: through-thickness resolution is not the cause.
Conclusion: the non-monotone trough series is a property of the observable plus a tied mesh family, not evidence of a defect; analyse correctly refuses to extrapolate it.

## 6. Interpretation claims

1. "Floor not specific to s4f". Supported in the restricted sense: a second, structurally different continuum discretisation (monolithic P2/P1/P2 ALE, tight tolerance) shows the same same-dt 0.5% P L-inf difference from oomph with the error series correlated at 0.85, and sits 0.14-0.28% P from s4f F4. Alternatives not excluded: (a) beam vs continuum model form: FE and s4f are both plane-strain continuum, oomph is a Kirchhoff-Love beam, so a shared difference to oomph is exactly what model form predicts; COL-2's dry-wall gap (0.10-0.25% P) has the size of the dt-independent -0.15% P trough offset; MF1/H6 is the only discriminating test and was not run at 37e6af0; (b) oomph spatial bias 0.26% P unremoved (U_ref 0.30); (c) shared definitions (ramp, start-up from Poiseuille with zero wall velocity, rho_s regularisation, normalisation by the same reference peak, outlet/inlet treatment: same author line, FE built to replicate the tutorial); (d) the s4f data are one run. 2. "Space and time separable": supported at trough level (additivity ratio <= 4% of the time effect) and, by my history-level X, for S2/S3 at <= 0.016% P; the registered 0.05% P threshold is vacuous as stated.
