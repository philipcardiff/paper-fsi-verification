# PREREG COL-2 amendment A1 (2026-11-06, written after the F1 full run and the F2 smoke test (4 steps, tiny meshes), before the F2 full run)
Smoke observation: with a moving wall the wall-normal pressure variation across rows is physical (the fluid under the wall accelerates vertically: p_wall - p_floor = 0.5 rho A''(t) H, order 1/60 = 1.7-3% of the added-mass pressure p ~ rho L_eff A''/H), so the preregistered literal row-spread gate (<= 3% of mean) is not a clean parity test for F2. The literal gate stays as registered and is reported literally. ADDED diagnostics (not replacing any gate): the analytic physical row difference Appred = 0.5 A''(t) H from the spline second derivative of the prescribed amplitude, the observed p_wall - p_floor, and the parity amplitude A_par (the registered parity statistic, unchanged, which removes the smooth part). The mechanism verdict (H2) is carried by A_par/p <= 3% and S <= 3% (registered) AND |(p_wall - p_floor) - Appred| <= 3% of mean |p| (new, informative; its failure would be reported but cannot by itself change the H2 gate list).
