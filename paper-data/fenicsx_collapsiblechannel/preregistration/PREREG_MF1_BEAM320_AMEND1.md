# PREREG_MF1_BEAM320_AMEND1 (2026-11-11), written after the first 320-element run failed its acceptance test A2 and before the rerun

Parent: PREREG_MF1_BEAM320.md (sha256 c871c6e5d1b65db17fc62c96c3ae460368ebf40790926f7fbed58e18ccea549c), unmodified.

## What happened
Job 18904 (ne = 320, Newton tolerance 1e-5 on the residual 2-norm, 30 iterations, as in the original replay) completed all 240 steps but every step used the full 30 iterations and was flagged not converged: test A2 failed. The result file is retained as `replay_MF1_beam320_FAILED_tol1e-5_run18904.json` and is not used.
Diagnostic jobs 18907 (ne = 160) and 18908 (ne = 320), same code with the residual and the Newton update recorded at every iteration (no other change; the ne160 diagnostic reproduces the stored 160-element history bit for bit; the ne320 diagnostic reproduces job 18904 bit for bit): at 320 elements the residual falls to 1.0-1.6e-5 after 3 iterations and then stays there (minimum per step 1.03e-5 to 1.37e-5, median 1.22e-5) while the Newton update is 4e-14 m (machine level). The state is converged to roundoff and the absolute tolerance 1e-5 is below the assembly floor at this resolution, as the COL-2 amendment A3 found for the 80-element beam (floor then 1.5e-6; 160 elements: final residuals 4.6e-6 to 9.3e-6, just under 1e-5). This is a property of the stopping criterion, not of the physics or of the model.
## Amendment (applies to the rerun of ne = 320 and to the ne = 160 control only)
Convergence criterion: residual 2-norm < 1e-4 (above the measured floor 1.0-1.6e-5 by a factor of 6-10) AND max-norm of the last Newton update < 1e-10 m (the observed converged updates are 4e-14 m); 30 iterations maximum; all other settings, loads, operator, observation points, definitions and the classification of section 4 of the parent are unchanged. The tolerance is chosen from the measured floor and not from the agreement with any other result.
Control: ne = 160 rerun with the same amended criterion must reproduce the stored 160-element history (max |dY| <= 1e-9 m); otherwise the amended criterion is not used for interpretation.
A2' (validity): every step satisfies the amended criterion within 30 iterations and all 240 steps are done. Expected cost: about one minute of wall per run on 2 ranks (0.05 CPU-h); the failed run and the two diagnostics cost 0.2 + 0.2 + 0.003 CPU-h; no other run is made.
