# PREREG_COLLAPSIBLE_PRODUCTION_AMEND2 (Agent COL-P, 2026-11-07): MF1 open-loop beam-vs-continuum replay in the coupled transient

Frozen parent PREREG_COLLAPSIBLE_PRODUCTION.md (sha256 3b92a32f...), unmodified; AMEND1 covers stage 3 and records that MF1 / H6 was not executed because no replay driver existed and only every 10th wall pressure was stored. This amendment, committed BEFORE the MF1 runs, authorises exactly one bounded study (budget <= 1.0 CPU-h; coordinator authorisation) and does not change any earlier result.

1. Hypothesis H6' (the registered H6 in its replay form): the open-loop gap between a Kirchhoff-Love beam (oomph equations, COL-2 code) and the 2-D plane-strain continuum wall, both driven by the SAME load history taken from the coupled S2 dt 0.00625 run, is <= 0.15 % P (P = 0.216 m; 0.15 % P is the COL-2 interpretation limit). Falsifier: gap > 0.15 % P.
2. Load history. ONE S2 cell (ny 16, wall 160x4, dt 0.00625, T = 1.5, natural ends, d2dt2 wall) is rerun as a separate Slurm job (2 ranks, nothing reused from stored every-10th data) and, every step at the converged state before the history shift, the fluid residual vector R_f(phi_i) at every interface u dof (x and y component; P2: 321 nodes x 2) is stored (g_i; the fluid force on the wall is -g_i, because the coupled system is R_s(phi_i) + R_f(phi_i) = 0 on the wall velocity rows). Also stored every step: pExt at the end-of-step time, the interface displacement d_x, d_y at the interface nodes, wall_d (bottom) and midplane d at x = 7.5, 10, 12.5 (the existing driver outputs). The traction density per reference length is t = M^-1 g with M the P2 mass matrix of the undeformed interface (exact reproduction of the nodal forces by the weak form; the P2 traces are exact on the straight reference interface). Pressure and shear are not separated: t carries the full fluid load (normal and shear) as a dead load, i.e. the load history is prescribed, no feedback.
3. Replay (a), continuum: wall-only submesh of the same mesh (identical triangulation), the same TL StVK form, clamped end faces, pExt Nanson follower on the outer face at the end-of-step time, the same time operators (BE first step; BDF2 velocity from d; wall inertia BDF2 of that velocity = d2dt2, histories of d and u), the stored traction as ds(interface) term. Control C2: the replayed continuum must reproduce the coupled wall_d history (bottom, three x, every step) with max|difference| <= 1e-6 x max|d_coupled|; if it does not, the beam is NOT interpreted (stop). Control C1: the rerun must reproduce the stored S2 dt 0.00625 wall histories (<= 1e-8 relative to max|d|), else the stored cell is not the replay source and this is reported.
4. Replay (b), beam: the COL-2 Hermite Kirchhoff-Love beam (E_eff = E/(1-nu^2), h = 0.05, consistent translational mass rho h, no rotary inertia), copied unchanged except for (i) a dead distributed load f = -t (per reference length, x and y; interpolated from the P2 traction at the Gauss points) plus the follower pExt(t) (end-of-step time), (ii) the time operator. Primary: the same d2dt2 operator as the coupled wall (u = BDF2 derivative of q, acceleration BDF2 of u, BE first step), ne = 160 (one beam element per P2 wall element) and ne = 80 (resolution sensitivity). Diagnostic only (labelled, not entering any decision): oomph's Newmark average-acceleration on the same load history, ne = 160. The beam axis displacement Y is compared with the continuum midplane (y = 1.025) displacement (bottom face reported alongside).
5. Observables: V1, V2, V3 (first trough, rebound, second trough; Hermite or cubic interpolation of the replayed history, same extraction as the production analysis) and fixed-time values at x = 7.5, 10, 12.5; history L2 and L-inf of (beam - continuum)/P over t <= 1.5; gap G = max_t |Y_beam - d_cont,mid| / P at each x. Decision on G (beam ne 160, d2dt2): <= 0.15 % P pass; 0.15-0.30 % P comparison limited by model form; > 0.30 % P model-form-defining. If |G(ne 160) - G(ne 80)| > 0.05 % P the gap is unresolved at the 0.05 % P level and said so. The gap is then set against the H3 offset (FE time limit -100.202 vs oomph -100.001 at x = 10, 0.20 % P) as an open-loop magnitude comparison only.
6. Limits of an open loop: no feedback through the fluid. The wall-inertia part of a model difference (rotary and shear inertia) is suppressed in the coupled problem by the added mass (audit: added-mass ratio 660-1050 for mode 1), so the open-loop gap may overestimate that part; the quasi-static stiffness part (shear compliance, through-thickness stress) is not suppressed by added mass and may be amplified or attenuated through the pressure-deflection feedback; the sign and size of the closed-loop gap are NOT estimated here. The dead-load replay applies the recorded force vector at the bottom face of the continuum and on the axis of the beam (no lever arm). A small gap here would not by itself prove closed-loop equivalence.
7. Budget and stops: <= 1.0 CPU-h in total (rerun about 0.4-0.6 CPU-h, replay jobs < 0.1); Slurm limit 30 min for the 2-rank rerun, in-driver maxwall 1650 s; Newton failure (one retry as before) -> FAIL; Jmin < 0.2 -> FAIL; failed C1 or C2 -> stop and report; no relaunch of the same failure more than once; replay jobs are separate jobs (>= 2 ranks); beam Newton tolerance 1e-5 (COL-2 A3, assembly roundoff floor), cap 30 iterations, non-convergence reported.
