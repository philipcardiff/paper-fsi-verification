## Models (all fixed now)
Fluid: P2/P1 Taylor-Hood on triangles (structured, diagonal "right"), rho = 1, nu = 0.02, channel x in [0,25], y in [0,1] before motion; elastic section on the top wall 5 < x < 15; inlet p = 6, outlet p = 0 as natural traction conditions (grad form: (nu grad u - p I) n = -p_bc n), u_y = 0 strongly at inlet/outlet; no-slip walls; convective term kept.
Time: BDF2 (BE first step), ALE (DOF-wise time derivative, convective velocity u - w_mesh), Newton by SNES (rtol 1e-10).
Loads (net y force per unit depth on the elastic section, N/m, positive = fluid pushes wall outward/up): F1 = nodal wall-row P1 pressure x tributary length (the FEBio "applied nodal load"); F2 = element mean (wall row + row 1)/2 x tributary length; F3 = weak consistent reaction: minus the assembled momentum-residual rows (before BC treatment) of the wall velocity y-dofs, end vertices weighted 1/2 (includes pressure and viscous parts).
Row-parity statistic A_par = |mean_j (-1)^j D_j| / 4 with D_j = p_{j-1} - 2 p_j + p_{j+1} on the P1 vertex rows of a wall-normal line (recovers the amplitude a of p_j = a(-1)^j; O(h^2 p'') for smooth p). FEBio observed 58 Pa on mean 135 Pa at t = 0.25 s, x = 10.
Mesh motion: vertical only, scalar harmonic (constant diffusivity) extension on the reference mesh of the wall displacement (d_y on the elastic wall, 0 elsewhere; d_x = 0 is the exact harmonic solution).
