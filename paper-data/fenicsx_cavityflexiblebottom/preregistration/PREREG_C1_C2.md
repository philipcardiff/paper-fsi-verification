Loads (all applied on the top face only):
- U: uniform follower Cauchy pressure 1.315 Pa (= the load of `plate_laws.py`).
- F (primary load for the recorded floor): follower, Cauchy pressure p(X) on the DEFORMED surface (Nanson, t = -p J F^-T N), p from the 80 recorded face pressures, labelled by reference position X_i = 2.025 + 0.05 i (the faces are x-sorted and the floor has 80 faces of reference width 0.05), piecewise linear between face centres, constant beyond the end centres. Reason: the recorded p is a pressure on the deformed floor (face-area vectors from the deformed points) and the tutorial passes a deformed-normal pressure; this is the physical, benchmark-defined measure.
- DP: reference-configuration pressure, t = -p(X) N (fixed direction and per-reference-area density).
- DV: reference-configuration force vectors, the recorded face forces p_i Sf_i spread over the reference faces (t = p_i Sf_i / 0.05, includes the small Sf_x).
F vs DP/DV compares the two load measures. No linear geometry, no other material law.
