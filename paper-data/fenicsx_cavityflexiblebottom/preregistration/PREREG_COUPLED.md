## 3. Formulation and preflight (C4, coarsest level L1; evidence, all before this registration)
Monolithic ALE on one mesh, unknowns (u P2, p P1, d P2), TL StVK plane strain solid with the elastic virtual work P:grad(phi) shared with the fluid test function, BE in pseudo-time (theta = 1), interface velocity = (d1 - d0)/dt by the kinematic equation, harmonic fluid mesh motion, natural do-nothing outlet. Preflight results (`preflight_analytic_L1.json`, `preflight_L1_dt5/`, `rigid_L1_*/`, runs `cavpre_*`, `cavrigid_*`):
- T1 constant pressure p0: direct and weak total force on the plate (0, -4 p0) to 4e-14; viscous part 2e-14. The weak force without the clamp-shared corner dofs is 4.1/492 = 0.83 % lower; this equals the analytic corner share 2 p0 h/6 (the corner nodal forces go straight into the clamp) and is why the balance uses the exclusion while the physical force report uses the direct integral and the weak force on all dofs.
- T2 Nanson: int |J F^-T N| dS0 equals the deformed P2 interface length to 2e-15 (stretch 1.0086 for the prescribed field). T3 deformed constant-pressure force: 5e-15.
- Interface velocity equals (d1-d0)/dt at interface nodes to 1e-17 at every step; fluid discrete continuity int J div u = 1e-14; min fluid Jacobian 0.957 at the plate peak excursion of the pseudo-time transient.
- Assembled flag-style balance (left + right support reaction + weak fluid force on plate dofs (excluding clamp corners) - plate inertia): <= 3e-13 relative at every step.
- Power transfer: weak fluid power on the plate P_f equals the plate-side power P_s (inertia + stress power) to round-off at every step (the two sum rows vanish at the interface).
- Mass defect: the BE discrete-GCL defect is entirely the closed form -(1/dt) int J1 det(F1^-1 grad(d1-d0)) (mass_norm = geom_norm in the data); it peaks at 7.8e-4 of the inflow in the large-increment transient (dt = 10) and decays with the increments to <= 1e-12 at the steady state (L1 data), where d1 = d0 and the defect is identically zero. Absolute and normalised defects are reported for every step.

## 4. Choice of the time integrator (justified)
BE (theta = 1) pseudo-time, NOT BDF2 or CN: (i) the plate is undamped with a bending period of about 50 s (omega ~ 0.125 rad/s), so the steady state is reached only by the numerical dissipation of the integrator: BE removes the transient in ~20 steps at dt = 10, a second-order scheme would ring for hundreds of seconds; (ii) the steady state is independent of dt (fixed point of the scheme, verified above to 1.5e-5); (iii) the moving-mesh mass defect of BE (STAGE_C finding) is a transient property that vanishes at the fixed point, and is monitored. Inlet ramp 0.5 (1 - cos(pi t/20)) for t < 20 (as the quasi-monolithic reference; the steady state does not depend on it).
Production dt = 10 s: the largest tested dt with convergence, agreement with dt = 2.5 within 2e-5 relative in u_y and F_y, and the lowest cost (rule written now: largest of {2.5, 5, 10, 20} that converged and agrees within 1e-4 relative with the smallest). Fall-back rule: if any production level fails SNES at dt = 10, that level is rerun at dt = 5 and the failed run kept in the report.
Steady criterion: relative rate |change|/(|value| dt) < 1e-6 per second of both u_y(A) and the weak F_y, for 3 consecutive steps with t >= 60 s; maximum 600 s. Newton: SNES newtonls, MUMPS LU, rtol 1e-9, atol 1e-10 (the class defaults); tolerance sensitivity run S1 below.

## 6. Observables, validity gates and decision rules
Recorded every step (`history.csv`): u_x(A), u_y(A); interface force on the plate Fx, Fy total, pressure part, viscous part (direct reference-area Nanson integral = sigma_f n da), weak nodal force (excluding and including the clamp-corner dofs); left and right support reactions; balance residual (relative); P_f, P_s, cumulative interface work W_if, strain energy, kinetic energy; fluid volume, inflow, outflow, mass defect (absolute, normalised by |Q_in|) and its closed form; int J div u; min and max fluid Jacobian (DG1 and DG2 samples), min plate Jacobian; max |d|; interface-velocity error; floor mean pressure; Newton iterations, reason, final residual; drift rates; wall time; CPU-h from sacct.
Per-run validity gates (a run that fails one is reported FAILED/PARTIAL and excluded from the order analysis): G1 every SNES converged; G2 steady criterion reached; G3 minimum fluid Jacobian > 0 at every step (a value below 0.3 is flagged); G4 relative balance residual <= 1e-8; G5 |P_f - P_s| <= 1e-8 max(|P|, 1e-3); G6 interface-velocity error <= 1e-10; G7 |mass defect - closed form| / |Q_in| <= 1e-8 at every step (i.e. the defect is fully explained) and the steady-state (final) normalised defect <= 1e-9 (L1 data: 1e-13); G8 relative steady drift of u_y(A) and F_y <= 1e-6 per s.
Spatial: `verification/convergence.py::analyse` with h = dx (refinement ratio 2) for u_y(A), u_x(A), F_y (total, pressure), weak F_y, support reaction: an order and an extrapolation are reported ONLY if accepted (>= 3 valid levels, monotone, 0.5 <= p <= 3); non-monotone data get no order and no Richardson value. With four levels both the (L2,L3,L4) and (L1,L2,L3) triples are printed.
Temporal (T1): negligible if |u_y| and |F_y| (L2, dt 5 vs 10) differ by <= 1e-4 relative; otherwise reported as a time error of that size. Nonlinear solver (S1): negligible if the L1 steady values differ <= 1e-6 relative.
Reference comparison: R = solids4foam partitioned-strict top-surface u_y = -0.2407 (independent re-derivation from the raw four-level CSV `<local path>`: -0.204568, -0.231445, -0.238404, -0.240113, orders 1.95 and 2.02, extrapolation recomputed in the results file) and F_y = -5.2607. Bands (written now): |Delta u_y|/|R| <= 0.3 % "agrees"; 0.3-1.0 % "agrees within the expected formulation band" (the tutorial uses the M2-type transfer; the audit proxy predicts an M2-minus-M1 effect of +0.5 to +1 % in |u_y|); > 1 % "discrepancy, to be explained". The comparison quantity is the extrapolated M1 value if accepted, else the finest level. PREDICTIONS written before the runs: (a) FEniCSx M1 u_y(A) at L4 lies between -0.2380 and -0.2407; (b) the M2 sensitivity changes |u_y| at L2 by +0.3 to +1.2 % relative to M1 at L2, moving the FEniCSx value toward R; (c) F_y within 0.5 % of -5.2607 at the finest level; (d) |u_x(A)| < 1.1e-4 m (s4f 9.6e-5, ANSYS 4.1e-5). Tukovic (-0.2510), ANSYS (-0.2536), COMSOL (-0.2431) are quoted as model-equivalence-conditional context only (their plate laws are unverified; COMSOL plane stress/strain unknown); no pass/fail is attached to them.
Error classes reported separately: spatial (level differences), temporal (T1, scan), nonlinear solver (S1, residuals), reference-model uncertainty (s4f partitioned vs quasi-monolithic spread 0.05-0.16 %, strict vs legacy, COMSOL/ANSYS spread), model-form difference (M1 vs M2 exact vs tutorial transfer; mesh-motion diffusivity; outlet form; element/geometry).
If a mesh fails by deformation: diagnose the ALE cause (Jacobian field, location) BEFORE changing the mesh-motion algorithm, and verify any changed algorithm (rerun T1/T2 analytic and geometry checks) before more production.
CPU-h by `sacct -X -o Elapsed,AllocCPUS`; every job is ledgered by `slurm/submit_run.sh` (append only) in `CENTRAL_RESOURCE_LEDGER.csv` of this worktree; failed runs are kept.
